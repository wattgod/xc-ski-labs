## Deliverables

1. `scripts/generate_prep_kit.py` (this repo): XC prep-kit pages at
   `/race/{slug}/prep-kit/`. Content assembled ONLY from fields that
   actually exist in this repo's `race-data/` profiles — inventory the
   schema first and list in the recap which sections were possible.
   Target sections (include what data supports, in this order): race
   logistics (date, venue, distances, technique classic/skate), pacing
   guidance by distance, fueling outline (adapt effort-duration bands, not
   invented numbers), gear/wax-day checklist (generic checklist acceptable;
   NO fabricated wax recommendations — if wax detail isn't in race-data,
   ship a preparation checklist and say so), links back to the race page.
2. Gate on race pages: kit CTA + email unlock following the GG mechanic —
   submit → worker (`brand: xcskilabs`, `source: prep_kit_gate`,
   `race_slug`) → unlock/redirect to the kit page. Honeypot field like the
   gravel forms. Voice: deadpan-warm, no hype ("Leave your email — the
   {race_name} prep kit is yours.").
3. Season sanity: kits must render correctly for races with 2027 dates
   (most of the calendar right now) — weeks-out framing should never claim
   a race is "past".
4. Tests: kit generator renders for 3 representative races (Birkie + two
   others with thin data), gate HTML present on generated race pages,
   worker payload shape matches the gravel gate's payload byte-for-byte in
   field names.
