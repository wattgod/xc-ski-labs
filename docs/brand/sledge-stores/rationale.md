# How this was chosen

This section is background, not rules. When it and the book differ, the book wins.

## The path to Sledge Stores

1. **2026-11-13, four retro directions:** Wax Chart, Loppet Poster, Wax Room Bulletin and Polar Depot. A hybrid with Endure Glyph followed, then polish rounds. Matti chose **P1 Poster rim**.
2. **2026-11-13, a drafted brand book.** It was reviewed separately by Fable and Astra, whose fixes were folded in. Matti then kept the P1 lift and the crossed-skis logo.
3. **2026-11-14, logo studies.** Matti liked Astra's redraw of the crossed skis and asked for polar-expedition colours with salmon.
4. **2026-11-14, salmon studies.** Astra and Opus 5.5 each drew three directions from one brief. Matti picked Expedition Ledger and Sledge Stores, then saw each at four salmon levels, from primary to a splash. He chose **Sledge Stores · Splash on wool**.

## Trend fit

The 2026 sources read earlier still apply, with a different emphasis:
- **Heritage done with restraint:** one signature idea (the stores), real product truth, no costume ageing. The book bans sepia photos, explorer illustrations and expedition puns for that reason.
- **Tactility without skeuomorphic gloss:** stitches, slats and woven canvas, all flat, with no shadows.
- **Expressive display type over a calm body face:** a stencil for titles and prices, Libre Franklin for reading.

**Likely to date first:** the stencil face and the tilted shipping tags. **Durable:** the navy-on-wool base, one salmon splash, and big plain prices.
