# Separation sheet

Checked on 2026-11-14 against the live home pages of ten outdoor brands. Text colour, background colour and font family were read from each page's computed styles. Colour distance is CIEDE2000 (ΔE00): under about 2 is hard to see, and over 10 is clearly a different colour. Astra scored the same field separately, from memory, before seeing these numbers.

This sheet is about design only. It is not a trademark search, and no legal clearance of the name "XC Ski Labs" has been done.

## What is ours, and what is shared

- **Shared:** cream grounds, heritage outdoor mood, square corners and Norwegian ski context. Every one of these is in at least two other brands.
- **Ours:** mid fjord navy, salmon as the single marker, stencil display type, slatted crates and the two-colour crossed skis.
- **At risk only in combination:** wool weave, leather-brown rules, stitched edges, luggage tags and strap header together read as a clothing outfitter before they read as coaching. Astra flagged this as the costume risk.

## What changed in the book

The rules are in §13 Brand boundaries:
- The first screen says what XC sells.
- Skis never share a colour, and the mark never sits between words.
- The small-size mark is not specified yet.
- No knit patterns, heritage claims or clothing-led photos.
- Crates, tags and stitches stay in commerce.
- `splash-deep` stays to thin slats.

## Not checked

- Our own pages were not rendered next to theirs. The comparison is of sampled values and home-page screenshots.
- Only home pages were checked. Product pages, Amundsen's Cross-Country Skiing pages, emails, packaging and campaign graphics were not.
- Devold, Holubar, Patagonia Worn Wear, Snow Peak and Peak Performance appear only in Astra's memory scores; their sites were not opened.
- Roadie Labs: its palette has not been found, so no check.
- The name search is preliminary and US-only (see §13). It found NORDIC SKI LAB, a live registration for cross-country ski instruction videos. No design-mark search for the crossed skis.
