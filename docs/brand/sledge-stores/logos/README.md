# Logos

- `xc-mark-wool.svg`: the mark, wool colourway. A wool tile with one navy ski and one salmon ski (the salmon one runs from lower left to upper right) and wool bindings. Use it on straps and navy grounds.
- `xc-mark-fjord.svg`: the mark, fjord colourway. A navy tile with one wool ski and one salmon ski, and navy bindings. Use it on wool and oat grounds and for avatars.
- `xc-mark-small.svg`: the small mark for favicons and app icons up to 32px, approved 2026-11-14. Fjord tile, the skis crossing low in a herringbone V with hooked tips, wool on the left and salmon running lower left to upper right.
- `xc-crossed-skis.svg`: the previous drawing (cream skis on dark green). Retired 2026-11-15, when the small mark became the live favicon.

The drawing is Astra's redraw of the crossed skis (2026-11-14). Minimum size 24px for the main mark; up to 32px, favicons and app icons use the small mark. Clear space is one ski's width. Never place the mark on salmon or recolour it outside these two colourways.
