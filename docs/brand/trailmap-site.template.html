Point-to-point · 1,050 m climbing
Two feed stations shown

The Birken is not a technical race. It is a long one, in a place where the weather decides. Fifty-four kilometres from Rena, up onto the ridge at 900 metres where there is no shelter at all, then down into Lillehammer.

You carry 3.5 kilos the whole way. That is the race — not the distance, the pack, and the hour you spend above treeline in March carrying it.

Ten thousand people start and the tracks will be good; grooming here is as reliable as it gets anywhere. What ends days is the top. Skiers strong enough for the climb get cold on the plateau and lose the descent. Dress for the ridge, not for the start pen. If it is blowing hard the organisers move the route down, and you should be glad when they do.

Do not make this your first marathon.
