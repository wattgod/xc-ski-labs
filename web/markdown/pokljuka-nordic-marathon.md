## Course

World-class loops on the Pokljuka biathlon center trails, which hosted the 2021 IBU Biathlon World Championships. The course extends beyond the competition loops into the broader plateau forest. Dense spruce forest skiing at 1300-1400m with views of Triglav (2864m) and the Julian Alps from clearings.

## Climate

Alpine plateau winter

Pokljuka's elevated plateau at 1300-1400m in the Julian Alps delivers reliable snow from December through March. The dense forest cover provides excellent wind shelter. Temperatures range from -12°C to -2°C. The plateau's position catches precipitation from the Adriatic.

Typical race-day temperature: -12 to -2°C

Challenges:
- Dense fog can blanket the plateau suddenly
- Heavy snowfall events during Adriatic low-pressure systems
- Temperature inversions occasionally bring warm air above the cold plateau
- Icy conditions after rain-on-snow events

## History

Founded in 2003 to leverage Pokljuka's growing biathlon infrastructure, this marathon gained stature after the plateau hosted the 2021 IBU Biathlon World Championships. Slovenia has a strong winter sports tradition relative to its size, and Pokljuka represents the country's best Nordic terrain. The race draws participants from across the Alps region.

### Notable Facts

- Pokljuka hosted the 2021 IBU Biathlon World Championships
- The plateau is within Triglav National Park, Slovenia's only national park
- Triglav (2864m) is visible from the course — it appears on Slovenia's flag and coat of arms
- Pokljuka has hosted IBU Biathlon World Cup events since 1993
- Slovenia punches well above its weight in winter sports for a country of 2 million
