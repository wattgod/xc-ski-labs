## History

Founded in 1945 as an untimed, open-track version of Vasaloppet, held the day after the main race. Created to give everyday skiers the chance to experience the full 90km Vasaloppet course without qualification requirements or competitive timing. It has grown into one of the world's largest recreational ski events, attracting around 8,000 participants annually.

### Notable Facts

- Name means 'Open Track' in Swedish — anyone can enter without qualifying
- Held on Monday after Vasaloppet since 1945
- No timing, no results — purely experiential
- Many use it as their first Vasaloppet-distance experience before attempting the real race
- Same aid stations serve the iconic blueberry soup
- Part of Vasaloppet Week which includes 9 events over 8 days
