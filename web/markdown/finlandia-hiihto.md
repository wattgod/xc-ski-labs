## Course

Rolling loop course through the forests and lakeland terrain around Lahti, Finland's winter sports capital. The route uses the same trail network that has hosted multiple FIS World Championships. Moderate elevation changes with some punchy climbs and fast descents.

Format: loop

## History

Finlandia-hiihto was founded in 1974 in Lahti, Finland's undisputed winter sports capital. It rapidly grew to become one of the world's largest cross-country ski events, setting a Guinness World Record in 1984 with 13,226 participants. The race is a Worldloppet member and takes place on trail networks that have hosted multiple FIS Nordic World Ski Championships. While participation has declined from its 1980s peak, the event remains a cornerstone of Finnish ski culture and the Worldloppet circuit.

### Notable Facts

- Set the Guinness World Record in 1984 with 13,226 starters
- Lahti has hosted the FIS Nordic World Ski Championships 7 times
- One of the founding members of the Worldloppet series
- Finland is the birthplace of competitive cross-country skiing
- The Salpausselkä Games, held at the same venue, are a World Cup fixture
- Both classic and freestyle distances offered since the 1990s
