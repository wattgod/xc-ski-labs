Rolling loops through Pere Marquette State Forest on groomed trails. Mixed terrain with wooded singletrack, open meadows, and gentle climbs. Classic and freestyle courses share the same trail network.

Format: loop

## Climate

Great Lakes continental winter

February in northern Michigan delivers reliable cold with lake-effect snowfall. Temperatures typically range from -15°C to -3°C. Lake Michigan's proximity moderates extremes but feeds heavy snowfall. Wind chill on open sections can be severe.

Typical race-day temperature: -12 to -3°C

Challenges:
- Lake-effect snow can dump fresh powder overnight, softening trails
- Wind chill on exposed meadow sections
- Temperature swings from shaded forest to sun-exposed clearings
- Wax selection complicated by variable snow conditions

## History

Founded in 1977, the North American Vasa takes its name from the Swedish Vasaloppet, paying homage to Michigan's Scandinavian heritage. The race traverses the VASA Pathway in Pere Marquette State Forest outside Traverse City. Over nearly five decades it has become the Midwest's signature marathon ski event, drawing a passionate community of Nordic skiers from across the Great Lakes region.

### Notable Facts

- Named after the Swedish Vasaloppet, reflecting Michigan's Nordic roots
- Uses the VASA Pathway, one of the finest groomed trail systems in the Midwest
- Traverse City's cherry country provides an unexpected backdrop for Nordic skiing
- Has run continuously since 1977 with rare weather cancellations
- Offers both classic and freestyle events on the same weekend
