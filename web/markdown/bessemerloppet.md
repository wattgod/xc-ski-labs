## History

Founded in 1960 at Högbo Bruk, a historic ironworks in Sandviken, Sweden. Named after the Bessemer steel-making process that once made Sandviken a center of Swedish industry. The 52km classic race through the Gästrikland forests has over 65 years of history. Part of the Ski Classics Challengers series, it connects Sweden's industrial heritage with its skiing traditions.

### Notable Facts

- Named after the Bessemer process — Sandviken was a major steel-producing center
- Högbo Bruk dates to the 17th century — one of Sweden's oldest ironworks
- Founded in 1960, over 65 years of continuous history
- Sandviken is home to the Sandvik industrial corporation
- Part of the Ski Classics Challengers series
- One of Gästrikland's defining sporting events
