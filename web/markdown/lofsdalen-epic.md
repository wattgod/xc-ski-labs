## History

Lofsdalen Epic is one of the newer additions to the Swedish long-distance ski calendar, established in the 2010s in the mountain village of Lofsdalen, Härjedalen. Despite its relative youth, it has joined both the Ski Classics Challengers circuit and the Svenska Langlopscupen 'The Originals' series, establishing itself as an important late-season event.
