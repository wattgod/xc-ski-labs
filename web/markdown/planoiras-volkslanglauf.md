## Climate

Alpine valley winter

January in Lenzerheide at 1,500m means cold, reliable snow cover in the Graubunden Alps. Temperatures range from -15°C to -2°C. The valley position provides some shelter from extreme wind while the altitude ensures consistent winter conditions. January's deep snow provides excellent grooming base.

Typical race-day temperature: -15 to -2°C

Challenges:
- Mid-altitude Alpine cold — sub-zero temperatures common
- January daylight still limited in Swiss Alps
- Valley fog can reduce visibility
- Altitude at 1,500m — mild performance impact for lowland skiers

## History

Founded in 1986, the Planoiras Volkslanglauf is the traditional season-opening race of the Swiss Loppet series. Held in the deeply snow-covered Lenzerheide region of Graubunden, it has been a fixture of Swiss citizen skiing for four decades. The 41st edition takes place in 2026. The race reflects the Swiss tradition of well-organized Volkslanglauf (folk cross-country) events that emphasize participation and enjoyment of winter landscapes.
