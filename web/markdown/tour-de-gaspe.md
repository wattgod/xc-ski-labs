Multi-day stage race traversing the Gaspé Peninsula through boreal forest, with views of the Chic-Choc Mountains and the Gulf of St. Lawrence.

Format: stage

## Climate

Maritime subarctic

The Gaspé Peninsula experiences cold maritime winters with heavy snowfall from the Gulf of St. Lawrence. Temperatures typically -20°C to -5°C.

Typical race-day temperature: -20 to -5°C

Challenges:
- Remote wilderness with limited support access
- Maritime storms can bring whiteout conditions
- Multi-day exposure to cold requires expedition preparation
- Variable trail conditions in backcountry sections

## History

The Tour de Gaspé is a multi-day Nordic ski expedition traversing Quebec's remote Gaspé Peninsula. One of the few stage-format Nordic events in North America, it combines competitive skiing with wilderness adventure through the Chic-Choc Mountains.

### Notable Facts

- One of the few multi-day Nordic ski expeditions in North America
- Traverses the remote Gaspé Peninsula in eastern Quebec
- The Chic-Choc Mountains are the highest peaks in southern Quebec
- Expedition format attracts adventure-oriented Nordic skiers
