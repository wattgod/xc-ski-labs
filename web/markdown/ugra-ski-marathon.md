## Course

A flat 50km freestyle course on the Gornaya River floodplain in Khanty-Mansiysk, the oil-rich Khanty-Mansi Autonomous District. Mostly open terrain with minimal elevation change. The flat profile produces fast times and favors skating technique. The course is designed for both mass participation and elite racing.

## Climate

Siberian continental winter

Late March in Khanty-Mansiysk means deep continental cold with guaranteed snow cover. Temperatures range from -20°C to -2°C. Western Siberia's extreme continental climate provides the most reliable snow conditions of any marathon-distance race. Spring sun begins to warm but temperatures remain well below zero.

Typical race-day temperature: -20 to -2°C

## History

The Ugra Ski Marathon, founded in 2013 in Khanty-Mansiysk, is a major Russian citizen ski event on the flat Gornaya River floodplain. Set in the oil-rich Khanty-Mansi Autonomous District of Western Siberia, it has attracted Olympic and world champions alongside mass participation. The event was part of the Russialoppet Super Cup and briefly held Euroloppet membership (2015-2017). Since 2022, geopolitical isolation has limited international participation.

### Notable Facts

- Founded in 2013 in Khanty-Mansiysk, Western Siberia
- Has attracted Olympic and world champions
- Part of Russialoppet Super Cup
- Formerly Euroloppet member (2015-2017)
- Flat course on the Gornaya River floodplain
- Khanty-Mansiysk is a major biathlon and Nordic sports center
- International participation limited since 2022 due to geopolitical isolation
