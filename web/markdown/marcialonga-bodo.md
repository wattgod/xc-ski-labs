## History

Marcialonga Bodo is a relatively new addition to the Ski Classics calendar, launched as a Challengers event in 2024 and elevated to full Pro Tour status in 2025. The race brings the storied Marcialonga brand from the Italian Dolomites to the Arctic coast of Northern Norway, creating a unique hybrid of Italian racing tradition and Norwegian ski culture.

### Notable Facts

- Launched in 2024 as a Ski Classics Challengers event
- Elevated to full Ski Classics Pro Tour status in 2025
- Extends the Marcialonga brand from Italy to Arctic Norway
- One of the northernmost Ski Classics Pro Tour events
- 300-point category in the Pro Tour standings
