## Climate

Cascade rain shadow

Methow Valley sits in the rain shadow of the North Cascades, receiving reliable cold dry snow. Typically -15°C to -2°C in winter.

Typical race-day temperature: -15 to -2°C

Challenges:
- Cold temperatures in valley bottom
- Occasional warm Chinook winds
- Variable conditions across 200km network
- Remote valley access in winter

## History

The Race of the Methow is a signature racing event in Washington's Methow Valley, home to North America's largest groomed cross-country ski trail network with over 200km of trails. It serves as a Regional Junior Olympics Qualifier (JNQ) for the Pacific Northwest.

### Notable Facts

- Held on North America's largest groomed XC trail network
- Regional JNQ qualifier for Pacific Northwest
- Youth teams participate from across the western US
- Two-day format with classic and freestyle events
