## Climate

Northern New England mountain winter

Craftsbury Common sits in Vermont's coldest corner. January-February race dates deliver reliable cold, typically -18°C to -5°C. The Northeast Kingdom receives consistent natural snowfall supplemented by snowmaking at the Craftsbury Outdoor Center.

## History

Founded in 1981 at the Craftsbury Outdoor Center, the Craftsbury Marathon has grown into the largest Nordic ski marathon in the eastern United States. The Craftsbury Outdoor Center — established in 1976 — is one of America's premier cross-country ski training facilities, home to the Green Racing Project elite team. The marathon leverages this infrastructure to deliver a well-groomed course through Vermont's remote and beautiful Northeast Kingdom.

### Notable Facts

- The largest Nordic ski marathon in the eastern United States
- Hosted at the Craftsbury Outdoor Center, a US elite training facility
- The Green Racing Project elite team trains on the same trails year-round
- Craftsbury Common is one of the coldest inhabited places in Vermont
- The Center's snowmaking ensures coverage even in lean snow years
- Both classic and freestyle events offered
