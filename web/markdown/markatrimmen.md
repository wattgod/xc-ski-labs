## History

Markatrimmen is a classic Norwegian turrenn (citizen ski race) organized by Trondhjems Skiklub in Trondheim's beloved Bymarka forest. The race is a winter tradition for the Trondheim skiing community, representing the deep Norwegian culture of recreational long-distance skiing. Bymarka is one of Norway's most popular urban wilderness areas, providing Trondheim's 200,000+ residents with immediate access to hundreds of kilometers of ski trails.
