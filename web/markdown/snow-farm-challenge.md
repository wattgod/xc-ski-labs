## Course

A 42km race on the Snow Farm's extensive trail system at 1,600m in the Cardrona Valley. The course traverses high-country tussock grassland and sheltered gullies with panoramic views of the Southern Alps, Cardrona Valley, and Lake Wanaka in the distance. Snow Farm is the Southern Hemisphere's only dedicated Nordic ski area with over 55km of groomed trails.

Format: loop  
Surface: Machine-groomed trails at a dedicated Nordic centre; tussock high country

## Climate

Southern Alps alpine winter

August is mid-winter in New Zealand. Snow Farm at 1,600m in the Cardrona valley receives reliable snowfall from the Southern Alps weather patterns. Temperatures typically -5°C to 5°C. ## History

The Snow Farm Challenge is held at Snow Farm, the Southern Hemisphere's only dedicated Nordic ski area, located at 1,600m in the Cardrona Valley near Wanaka, New Zealand. Snow Farm was developed in the 1990s and has become an important off-season training venue for international Nordic teams from Europe and North America. The Challenge is the centerpiece event of New Zealand's small but passionate Nordic skiing community.
