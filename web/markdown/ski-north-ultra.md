## Course

A 100km classic-only ultra-distance race along Minnesota's North Shore of Lake Superior, using the Norpine, Cascade River State Park, and Bally Creek trail systems. Hilly terrain through boreal forest with views of Lake Superior. One of North America's very few 100km XC ski races. Classic technique only — no skating permitted.

## History

Ski North Ultra launched in 2025 as one of North America's very few 100km cross-country ski races. Set along Minnesota's iconic North Shore of Lake Superior, the classic-only event uses the Norpine, Cascade River State Park, and Bally Creek trail systems. The 100km distance fills a gap in the North American citizen racing calendar for ultra-endurance classic skiers seeking distances comparable to European ultra events.
