## History

Västgötaloppet emerged in the early 2000s at Lassalyckan in Ulricehamn, western Sweden. It has carved a niche as one of the few events offering both skate and classic marathons on consecutive days. The race serves as one of Sweden's largest seeding races for Vasaloppet and Tjejvasan, and joined the Ski Classics Challengers circuit.
