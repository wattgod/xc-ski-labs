## Course

Rolling highland course through dense subalpine forest on the Sovereign Lake and Silver Star trail network. Well-designed loops with a mix of gentle climbs and flowing descents through snow-laden spruce and fir forest.

Format: loop  
Surface: Machine-groomed trails through subalpine forest  
Technical rating: 3/5  
Grooming: Excellent grooming on one of BC's most extensive Nordic trail networks (105km total)

### Features

- Part of a 105km groomed trail network — one of the largest in Canada
- Deep reliable snowpack from Okanagan Highland precipitation
- Snow-draped subalpine forest scenery throughout
- Adjacent to Silver Star ski resort village for post-race amenities

## Climate

Interior British Columbia highland

The Okanagan highlands receive heavy snowfall from Pacific moisture hitting the Monashee Range. Race day temperatures typically range from -10°C to -2°C. The 1,600m altitude delivers consistent cold and deep snowpack, with conditions among the most reliable in western Canada.

Typical race-day temperature: -10 to -2°C

Challenges:
- Altitude at 1,600m can affect participants from lower elevations
- Heavy snowfall events can coincide with race weekend
- Cold temperatures combined with altitude make hydration critical
- Road access to the resort can be challenging in storm conditions

## History

Established in 2001 on the Sovereign Lake Nordic Centre trails near Silver Star Mountain Resort. The area has become one of British Columbia's premier Nordic skiing destinations thanks to its exceptional snowpack and extensive trail network. The loppet draws participants from across BC and Alberta, serving as a key event in the Okanagan Nordic racing calendar.

### Notable Facts

- Sovereign Lake Nordic Centre has over 105km of groomed trails
- The area receives an average of 7 meters of snowfall annually
- Silver Star village is one of BC's few ski-in, ski-out resort communities
- The trail network connects to the Sovereign to Silver Star point-to-point race route
