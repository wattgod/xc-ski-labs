## Course

A point-to-point epic through Pallas-Yllästunturi National Park, crossing three major fells between Ylläsjärvi and Levi. The course passes through pristine Arctic wilderness, above and below the treeline, with significant climbing on each fell crossing. One of Finland's most challenging marathon courses.

Format: point-to-point  
Surface: Machine-groomed tracks through national park terrain with fell crossings  
Technical rating: 4/5  
Grooming: Well-maintained Finnish national park trails, exposed sections can deteriorate in wind

### Features

- Crosses three major fells in Pallas-Yllästunturi National Park
- Above-treeline skiing with panoramic Arctic views
- Pristine wilderness — one of Europe's last true frontiers
- Finish at Levi ski resort with full facilities
- April date means spectacular spring light conditions

## Climate

Arctic subarctic winter

April in Finnish Lapland means spring snow conditions above the Arctic Circle. Temperatures range from -10°C to +5°C. Longer daylight hours but the fell crossings are fully exposed. Snow is virtually guaranteed at this latitude in April. Above-treeline sections face Arctic wind.

Typical race-day temperature: -10 to 5°C

Challenges:
- Arctic winds on exposed fell crossings
- Spring sun causes rapid snow transformation during the race
- Above-treeline sections with zero wind shelter
- Remote wilderness course limits rescue access

## History

Founded in 1985, the Ylläs-Levi Hiihto is one of Finnish Lapland's premier ski marathons. The 70km course through Pallas-Yllästunturi National Park represents some of the most challenging and scenic terrain in European marathon skiing. As a Euroloppet member event, it draws Nordic skiers seeking an authentic Arctic wilderness experience above the Arctic Circle.

### Notable Facts

- Course passes through Pallas-Yllästunturi National Park, Finland's third-largest
- Crosses three major fells — among the most vertical Euroloppet courses
- Entirely above the Arctic Circle at 67°N latitude
- April race date guarantees snow while offering long spring daylight
- Euroloppet member event
- Finish at Levi, one of Finland's largest ski resorts
