## History

The Owl Creek Chase is a popular citizen race in Aspen, Colorado, part of the Ute Cup Nordic Series of seven events through the winter. It takes advantage of Aspen's well-maintained Nordic trail network.

### Notable Facts

- Part of the Ute Cup Nordic Series (7 events)

- Freestyle format on Aspen Nordic trails
