## Course

Loop course through the Black Jack Cross Country ski area in the Kootenay mountains above Rossland. The course traverses subalpine forest with mountain views and varied terrain. At 1500m altitude, the snow quality is consistently excellent. Rolling hills with some steep pitches make this a technical and demanding 30km.

## History

The Black Jack Loppet is organized by the Black Jack Cross Country Ski Club in Rossland, BC. Rossland is better known for its alpine skiing at Red Mountain Resort, but the Nordic community at Black Jack has carved out a dedicated following. The high-altitude trails at 1500m in the Kootenay mountains offer some of the most reliable snow conditions in western Canada.
