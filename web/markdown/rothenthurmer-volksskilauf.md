## History

Part of the Swiss Loppet series, the Rothenthurmer Volksskilauf takes place through the protected Rothenthurm moor landscape in central Switzerland. The moor was preserved by a historic 1987 referendum.

### Notable Facts

- Set in the Rothenthurm moor, protected since a 1987 Swiss referendum
- Part of the Swiss Loppet series
- One of central Switzerland's most scenic Nordic venues
