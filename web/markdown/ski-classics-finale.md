## History

The Ski Classics Finale is the season-ending race of the Visma Ski Classics pro tour, the premier long-distance cross-country skiing circuit. Launched in 2011, Ski Classics brought professional team structure to marathon skiing. The finale determines the overall season champion, making it the most tactically charged race of the calendar.

### Notable Facts

- Determines the overall Ski Classics season champion
- Part of the Visma Ski Classics pro tour since 2011
- Host venue rotates — has been held in Scandinavia and the Alps
- Features professional team tactics rarely seen in other marathon ski races
- Prize money and championship points make it the highest-stakes single race on tour
