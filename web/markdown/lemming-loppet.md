## History

Founded in 2016, the Lemming Loppet is a newer Nordic ski marathon modeled after Sweden's Vasaloppet. Set in Germany's Black Forest, it brings the Scandinavian loppet tradition to Central Europe.

### Notable Facts

- Founded 2016 — one of Germany's newest ski marathons
- Modeled on Sweden's Vasaloppet format
- Black Forest has a long Nordic skiing tradition
