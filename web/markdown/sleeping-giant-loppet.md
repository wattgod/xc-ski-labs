## History

The Sleeping Giant Loppet has been a cornerstone of Ontario Nordic skiing since 1981, drawing skiers to Thunder Bay's boreal landscape along the shores of Lake Superior. Named for the iconic mesa formation visible from the city.

### Notable Facts

- Named for the Sleeping Giant mesa visible from Thunder Bay
- Established in 1981, one of Ontario's oldest loppets
- Thunder Bay is known as the Nordic skiing capital of Ontario
- Lake Superior provides dramatic backdrop to the course
