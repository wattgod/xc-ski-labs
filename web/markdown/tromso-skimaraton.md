## Climate

Arctic maritime

Tromsø sits at 69°N, well above the Arctic Circle. Spring conditions in April bring long daylight hours but cold temperatures. Maritime influence moderates extremes but brings variable weather.

Typical race-day temperature: -10 to -1°C

Challenges:
- Arctic wind exposure above the treeline
- Rapidly changing weather at high latitude
- Wind chill can be severe on exposed plateaus
- Late-season spring sun can soften snow surfaces

## History

The Tromsø Skimaraton is a ski marathon held in the mountains above Tromsø in Arctic Norway. One of the northernmost organized ski marathons in the world, it takes skiers through dramatic above-treeline terrain with views across the fjords and Arctic peaks of northern Troms.

### Notable Facts

- One of the northernmost ski marathons in the world at 69°N
- Course runs largely above the Arctic treeline
- Held in April when Tromsø has nearly 24 hours of daylight
- Views across Arctic fjords and the Lyngen Alps
