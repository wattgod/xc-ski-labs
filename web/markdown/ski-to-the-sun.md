## Course

Point-to-point through the Methow Valley trail system, traversing forest, meadow, and river valley terrain. Finishes with a climb up to Sun Mountain Lodge at 3,000 feet with panoramic views of the North Cascades.

Format: point-to-point  
Surface: Machine-groomed trails on North America's largest cross-country ski trail network  
Technical rating: 3/5  
Grooming: Excellent grooming standard, both classic and skate lanes maintained by Methow Trails

## Climate

Pacific Northwest mountain winter

The Methow Valley sits in the rain shadow of the Cascades, receiving reliable dry snowfall. Race day temperatures typically range from -10°C to 0°C. Blue skies are common, but Arctic outflow events can bring extreme cold.

Typical race-day temperature: -8 to 0°C

Challenges:
- Possible Arctic outflow creating bitter cold and wind
- Afternoon sun softening trails at lower elevations
- Variable wax conditions across elevation changes
- Remote valley location complicates logistics if weather turns

## History

Founded in 2019, Ski to the Sun is a relatively new race that leverages the Methow Valley's extraordinary trail infrastructure. The Methow Trails system, maintained by a dedicated nonprofit, is the largest groomed cross-country ski trail network in North America with over 200 kilometers of trails. The race quickly gained a cult following, selling out annually as word spread about the spectacular point-to-point course finishing at the iconic Sun Mountain Lodge.

### Notable Facts

- The Methow Valley has over 200 km of groomed trails — the largest network in North America
- Sells out every year despite the race's youth
- Sun Mountain Lodge finish provides one of the most scenic race endings in Nordic skiing
- The Methow Trails nonprofit maintains the entire network through community support
- The valley sits in the Cascade rain shadow, creating reliable cold, dry snow
