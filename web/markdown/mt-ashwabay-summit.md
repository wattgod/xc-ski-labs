## Climate

Lake Superior winter

February on the Bayfield Peninsula benefits from Lake Superior's snow belt effect. Temperatures typically -16°C to -4°C. Heavy lake-effect snow ensures deep snowpack. The peninsula's exposed position can bring significant wind chill from the lake.

Typical race-day temperature: -16 to -4°C

Challenges:
- Lake Superior wind chill on exposed sections
- Deep cold in February — frostbite risk
- Lake-effect snow squalls can reduce visibility
- Remote Bayfield Peninsula location requires planning

## History

The Mt. Ashwabay Summit is a skiathlon event on Wisconsin's Bayfield Peninsula, overlooking Lake Superior and the Apostle Islands. Mt. Ashwabay is a community-owned ski area that has served the Bayfield area since the 1960s. The skiathlon format — combining classic and skate technique in a single race — tests versatile Nordic skills in one of the snowiest areas of the upper Midwest.

### Notable Facts

- Skiathlon format combines classic and skate technique in one race
- Mt. Ashwabay is a community-owned ski area since the 1960s
- Bayfield Peninsula overlooks the Apostle Islands National Lakeshore
- The Apostle Islands are a US National Lakeshore with sea caves and historic lighthouses
- Bayfield is known as the gateway to the Apostle Islands
- Lake Superior's snow belt delivers over 100 inches of snow to the peninsula annually
