## Climate

Baltic winter

February in Estonia's Virumaa brings cold continental conditions tempered by Baltic maritime influence. Temperatures typically -10°C to -2°C. Snow reliability is moderate — the Baltic climate can produce mild spells, though the inland Virumaa location is more reliable than the coast.

Typical race-day temperature: -10 to -2°C

Challenges:
- Baltic thaw risk — maritime air masses can bring above-freezing temperatures

## History

The Viru Maraton is part of the Estoloppet series, Estonia's national circuit of long-distance ski marathons. Held in the Virumaa region of northeastern Estonia, the race traverses the ancient forests and raised bogs that characterize the Baltic landscape. Classic technique only, honoring the traditional style of Nordic skiing.

### Notable Facts

- Part of the Estoloppet series — Estonia's national ski marathon circuit
- Classic technique only — one of few marathon-distance classic-only events in the Baltics
- Virumaa is one of Estonia's most forested regions
- Estonia's raised bogs are unique European ecosystems
- Estonian skiing tradition dates to the medieval period
- The Estoloppet series connects ski communities across Estonia
