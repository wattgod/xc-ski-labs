## Course

Point-to-point from the valley town of Aurdal climbing toward the mountain resort of Beitostølen. The course follows traditional ski trails through the Valdres valley with a gradual net climb, passing forests and open mountain terrain beneath the Jotunheimen massif. The final kilometers offer panoramic mountain views.

Format: point-to-point  
Surface: Machine-groomed classic tracks through valley and mountain terrain  
Technical rating: 3/5  
Grooming: Good Norwegian standard, Langløpscupen Innlandet quality

## Climate

Mountain valley winter

March race date in the Valdres valley means cold but lengthening daylight. The Jotunheimen mountains shelter the valley but temperatures can still drop well below zero. Typically -12°C to -3°C. Snow is reliable at this altitude and time of year.

Typical race-day temperature: -12 to -3°C

Challenges:
- Cold mountain winds descending from Jotunheimen
- Variable snow conditions as March sun warms exposed sections
- Altitude gain in later kilometers adds fatigue
- Long point-to-point format demands pacing discipline

## History

A classic-technique marathon through the Valdres valley in central Norway, running from Aurdal to the mountain resort of Beitostølen. Part of the Langløpscupen Innlandet regional long-distance series. The race showcases some of Norway's most dramatic mountain scenery beneath the Jotunheimen range, Norway's highest mountain massif.

### Notable Facts

- Finishes at Beitostølen, one of Norway's premier Nordic skiing centers
- The Jotunheimen massif includes Galdhøpiggen, Scandinavia's highest peak
- Part of Langløpscupen Innlandet, the regional long-distance cup
- Valdres is one of Norway's traditional mountain districts
- Net uphill course from valley floor toward mountain plateau
