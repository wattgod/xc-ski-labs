## Course

Point-to-point on the American Birkebeiner Trail from Cable to Seeley. Rolling terrain through dense Northwoods forest with steady climbs and fast descents. The same legendary trail used for the Birkie, but in the classic-only format that rewards pure technique.

Format: point-to-point  
Surface: Machine-groomed classic tracks on the American Birkebeiner Trail  
Technical rating: 3/5  
Grooming: Excellent grooming by the Birkebeiner Trail crew, classic tracks set with skating lane

## History

Founded in 1992 as a classic-technique-only event on the American Birkebeiner Trail, the Seeley Hills Classic has grown into the second-largest annual classic ski race on the trail. It fills a niche for skiers who prefer the traditional stride over skating, offering a pure classic experience on some of the best-groomed trails in North America. The event serves as an early-season test before the February Birkie.

### Notable Facts

- Second-largest classic event on the Birkebeiner Trail after the Birkie itself
- Classic-only format attracts technique purists from across the Midwest
- Uses the same trail infrastructure as the American Birkebeiner
- Early-season timing makes it a key fitness benchmark for Birkie racers
- Seeley is a tiny Northwoods community that comes alive for skiing each winter
