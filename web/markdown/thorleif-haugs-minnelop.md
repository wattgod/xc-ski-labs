Forest circuit through the Finnemarka wilderness area west of Drammen. Rolling, hilly terrain through dense Norwegian forest. The course features genuine elevation changes with climbs and technical descents on classic tracks. Named after Thorleif Haug, who dominated the 1924 Chamonix Olympics.

## History

Founded in 1966 to honor Thorleif Haug (1894–1934), who won three gold medals in cross-country skiing and Nordic combined at the inaugural 1924 Winter Olympics in Chamonix. The 50km classic race through the Finnemarka forests near his home region has become one of Norway's most respected memorial ski marathons. Part of the Ski Classics Challengers series.

### Notable Facts

- Honors Thorleif Haug — triple gold medalist at the 1924 Chamonix Olympics
- Haug won the 18km, 50km, and Nordic combined at the first Winter Games
- Founded in 1966, over 60 years of continuous history
- Finnemarka is a popular wilderness area for Oslo-region skiers
- Part of the Ski Classics Challengers series
- One of the most technically demanding forest courses in Norway
