## History

The Larch Hills Loppet is a community-driven Nordic ski event in the Shuswap region of British Columbia. The Larch Hills trail system is maintained by volunteers and offers some of the best Nordic skiing in BC's interior.

### Notable Facts

- Larch Hills trail system is one of BC's best community-maintained Nordic areas
- Shuswap region has a strong Nordic skiing tradition
- Volunteer-run event with dedicated local community
