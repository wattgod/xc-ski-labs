## Climate

Alpine plateau winter

February on the Savoie Grand Revard plateau at 1,400m means reliable snow cover and moderate Alpine cold. Temperatures range from -10°C to 0°C. The plateau's exposure to sun creates pleasant racing conditions but can soften snow on south-facing sections.

Typical race-day temperature: -10 to 0°C

Challenges:
- Altitude effects at 1,400m — moderate performance impact
- Sun exposure on the open plateau can affect wax and snow conditions
- Wind exposure on plateau sections
- Variable snow quality between shaded forest and open terrain

## History

La Savoyarde is an established international Nordic skiing marathon held at La Feclaz on the Savoie Grand Revard plateau. The race combines cross-country skiing competition with a festival atmosphere, attracting recreational skiers, gliding enthusiasts, and serious competitors. It is part of both the Marathon Ski Tour circuit and Ski Classics Challengers calendar, giving it visibility beyond France's borders.

### Notable Facts

- Part of the Marathon Ski Tour — France's premier XC ski marathon circuit
- Ski Classics Challengers event — connects to the international pro racing calendar
- Held on the Savoie Grand Revard plateau, one of France's most scenic Nordic areas
- Combines competitive racing with recreational participation
- La Feclaz is one of the French Alps' premier Nordic ski centers
- 2026 winners: Victor Cullet Calderini (men) and Camille Coupe (women)
