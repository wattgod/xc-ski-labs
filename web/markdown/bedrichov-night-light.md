## History

The Bedrichov Night Light Marathon is an atmospheric evening race held on the same Jizera Mountains trail network that hosts the legendary Jizerska 50. Racing by headlamp through snow-covered spruce forests, it serves as both the traditional SkiTour season opener and the atmospheric prelude to the Jizerska 50 weekend. The night format creates a unique experience that sets it apart from conventional daytime marathons.

### Notable Facts

- Night race format — skiers race by headlamp and moonlight
- Uses the same trail network as the Worldloppet Jizerska 50
- Traditional CEZ SkiTour series opener
- Held in the Jizera Mountains, a memorial to 15 Czech mountaineers lost in Peru (1970)
- The darkness transforms familiar Jizerska trails into an entirely different experience
- Two distance options: 30km and 17.5km
