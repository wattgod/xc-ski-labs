## Course

Point-to-point through the Finnish lakeland from Niinisalo to Tampere, predominantly flat terrain through boreal forest and across frozen lakes. The 90km distance makes this a true ultra-marathon on skis.

Format: point-to-point  
Surface: Machine-groomed tracks through forest and frozen lake terrain  
Technical rating: 2/5  
Grooming: Finnish standard grooming, well-maintained with 12 service points

### Features

- 90km main distance — one of the longest Worldloppet races
- 12 fully-stocked service points along the course
- Finishes in Tampere, Finland's third-largest city
- Classic Finnish lakeland landscape — forest, lakes, silence
- Both classic and skate techniques available

## Climate

Continental Finnish winter

Finland's interior delivers consistently cold, stable February conditions. Temperatures range from -20°C to -5°C. The flat, forested terrain provides some wind shelter but frozen lake crossings expose skiers. Snow depth is typically excellent — Finland's continental climate produces dry, fast powder.

Typical race-day temperature: -15 to -5°C

Challenges:
- Sustained cold over 90km demands layering strategy
- Flat terrain means constant self-pacing with no downhill recovery
- Wind exposure on frozen lake sections
- Fueling across 12 service points requires nutrition planning

## History

Founded in 1955, Pirkan Hiihto is Finland's premier marathon ski race, covering 90 kilometers from the garrison town of Niinisalo to the city of Tampere. The race was established to promote long-distance skiing in the Pirkanmaa region and has grown to approximately 3,000 participants. With 12 service points, it is one of the best-supported marathon courses in Nordic skiing. The event embodies Finland's deep cultural relationship with cross-country skiing.

### Notable Facts

- One of the longest races in the Worldloppet series at 90km
- 12 service points — among the most supported courses in marathon skiing
- Finland's flagship marathon ski race since 1955
- The course from Niinisalo to Tampere crosses classic Finnish lakeland
- Strong domestic participation from Finland's large recreational skiing community
