## Course

A 76km point-to-point race through the forests and rolling terrain of Västerbotten in northern Sweden. The name '7-Mila' refers to seven Swedish miles (one Swedish mile = 10km). The course winds through classic Norrland landscapes — dense boreal forest, frozen lakes, and quiet inland terrain. Predominantly flat to gently rolling, favoring endurance over technical skill.

Format: point-to-point

## Climate

Northern Swedish winter

March in Västerbotten is still deep winter. At 64°N, temperatures remain well below freezing with reliable snow. Typically -15°C to -5°C. The northern latitude ensures excellent snow conditions well into spring, though daylight is increasing by March.

Typical race-day temperature: -15 to -5°C

Challenges:
- Extreme cold in northern Sweden in March
- 76km distance demands careful energy management in cold conditions
- Remote location with limited shelter along the course
- Variable light conditions at northern latitude

## History

Founded around 1980, 7-Mila is a 76km classic race in Robertsfors, Västerbotten, in northern Sweden. The name refers to seven Swedish miles (each Swedish mile being 10 kilometers). The race traverses the boreal forests of Norrland and has become a cherished event for the region's dedicated skiing community. While it lacks the international profile of the major Swedish races, it offers an authentic northern wilderness experience.
