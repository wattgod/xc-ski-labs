## History

The Avacha Marathon is held near Petropavlovsk-Kamchatsky on Russia's Kamchatka Peninsula, one of the most volcanically active regions on Earth. The race takes its name from the Avacha volcano visible throughout the course.

### Notable Facts

- Kamchatka has over 160 volcanoes, 29 of them active
- Avacha volcano last erupted in 2001
- Most remote event in the Russialoppet series
- Kamchatka is a UNESCO World Heritage Site for its volcanic landscape
