## Climate

Baltic winter

February in Lääne-Virumaa brings cold Baltic winter conditions. Temperatures typically -10°C to -2°C. Snow reliability is moderate — the inland location is somewhat more reliable than the coast but still subject to Baltic thaw events.

Typical race-day temperature: -10 to -2°C

Challenges:
- Baltic thaw risk — maritime air can disrupt snow conditions
- Flat terrain demands sustained classic technique endurance
- Low altitude provides no snow guarantee from elevation
- Smaller field can mean less draft assistance in classic tracks

## History

The Tamsalu Ski Marathon is a classic-technique event in the Estoloppet series, held in Lääne-Virumaa, northern Estonia. A community-focused marathon that connects Estonian skiers to the national long-distance skiing tradition. The Estoloppet series provides structure and organization for Estonia's citizen skiing calendar.
