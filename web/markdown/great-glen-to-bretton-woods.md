## Course

An ambitious point-to-point traverse connecting Great Glen Trails at the base of Mount Washington to the Bretton Woods Nordic Center. The route follows a mix of groomed trails and backcountry connections through the Presidential Range, with substantial climbing and descending through New Hampshire's most dramatic mountain scenery.

Format: point-to-point  
Surface: Mix of groomed trails and backcountry connectors  
Technical rating: 3/5  
Grooming: Variable — groomed sections at trail centers, backcountry-style connectors between

### Features

- Point-to-point traverse through the Presidential Range
- Views of Mount Washington and the Northern Presidentials
- Mix of maintained trail center tracks and wilder backcountry skiing
- Challenging terrain with significant climbing and descending
- Finishes at Bretton Woods Nordic Center with views of Mount Washington Hotel

## Climate

Northern New England mountain winter

Early March in the White Mountains brings highly variable conditions. Temperatures can range from -20°C to 5°C. Mountain weather is unpredictable, with wind and whiteout conditions possible on exposed sections.

Typical race-day temperature: -15 to 5°C

Challenges:
- Mountain weather can change rapidly — wind and whiteout conditions possible
- Trail conditions vary from groomed to backcountry-style
- Significant altitude gain and loss through the traverse
- Limited bail-out points on the remote sections

## History

A relatively new event that takes advantage of the trail systems at both Great Glen and Bretton Woods Nordic Centers, connected by backcountry routes through the White Mountains. The race celebrates New Hampshire's Nordic skiing heritage and the dramatic mountain landscapes of the Presidential Range.

### Notable Facts

- One of the few point-to-point Nordic races in the northeastern United States
- Route passes through some of the most dramatic mountain scenery in New England
- Mount Washington, the highest peak in the northeastern US, dominates the views
- Trail conditions can range from machine-groomed to full backcountry
