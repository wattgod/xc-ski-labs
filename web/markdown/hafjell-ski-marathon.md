## Course

A 42km skate race through the hills and forests above Øyer in the Gudbrandsdalen valley. The course uses the extensive trail network around Hafjell, a venue known for alpine skiing but with excellent cross-country infrastructure. Rolling terrain with meaningful climbs makes this a demanding skate marathon. The course rewards strong V1 climbing and confident downhill skating.

## History

Founded around 1990, the Hafjell Ski Marathon is Norway's longest dedicated skate/freestyle cross-country race at 42km. Based in Øyer, near Lillehammer in the Gudbrandsdalen valley, it uses the trail network around Hafjell — a venue that hosted events during the 1994 Winter Olympics. The race serves as a Birkebeiner seeding event and has become the premier skate marathon in a country dominated by classic-technique races.

### Notable Facts

- Norway's longest dedicated freestyle/skate race at 42km
- Official Birkebeiner seeding race
- Hafjell hosted freestyle skiing and biathlon during the 1994 Lillehammer Olympics
- Located in the Gudbrandsdalen valley — the historic Birkebeiner heartland
- One of the few Norwegian marathons focused exclusively on skate technique
- Part of the extensive Lillehammer-region skiing infrastructure
