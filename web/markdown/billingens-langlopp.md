## History

Billingens Långlopp has been part of the western Swedish ski calendar since approximately the 1980s. Named after the Billingen table mountain, a distinctive geological feature of the Västergötland landscape, the race is part of both the Ski Classics Challengers circuit and the Svenska Langlopscupen 'The Originals' series.
