A 30km course along the historic Rennsteig ridgeway, the longest continuously signposted long-distance ski trail in Central Europe at 142km total. The race section passes through the dense Thuringian Forest at 500-980m altitude, combining forested trail skiing with open ridge sections offering views across central Germany.

## Climate

Central European low-mountain winter

February in the Thuringian Forest at 500-980m means variable conditions. Temperatures range from -10°C to 2°C. Snow reliability is moderate — the Rennsteig ridgeway benefits from its elevation but climate change has reduced consistent cover in recent decades. Oberhof's biathlon infrastructure ensures excellent grooming when snow permits.

Typical race-day temperature: -10 to 2°C

Challenges:
- Variable snow conditions — low-mountain altitude makes coverage inconsistent
- Wind exposure on the open Rennsteig ridgeway
- Temperature fluctuations can create icy or slushy patches
- Fog common in the Thuringian Forest

## History

The Rennsteig Ski-Lauf is the winter counterpart to the famous Rennsteiglauf trail running race, following the historic Rennsteig ridgeway through the Thuringian Forest. The Rennsteig is the longest continuously signposted long-distance ski trail in Central Europe at 142km. Oberhof, the race base, is Germany's premier winter sports center and home to the biathlon World Cup venue, providing excellent infrastructure for the event.

### Notable Facts

- Winter counterpart to the famous Rennsteiglauf running race
- Set along the Rennsteig — the longest signposted ski trail in Central Europe (142km)
- Based in Oberhof, Germany's premier biathlon and winter sports center
- Euroloppet partner event
- Offers both classic and freestyle distances
- The Thuringian Forest has centuries of skiing heritage
