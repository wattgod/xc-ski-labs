## History

Ski de She is a women-only cross-country ski marathon held near Cable, Wisconsin, in the heart of the American Birkebeiner trail system. The event was created to provide a dedicated space for women in distance Nordic skiing, addressing the underrepresentation of women in citizen ski marathons. Held in the Chequamegon National Forest, it benefits from the trail infrastructure built for the nearby American Birkebeiner.

### Notable Facts

- Women-only event — one of very few women's-exclusive ski marathons in North America
- Located near Cable, WI — heart of the American Birkebeiner trail system
- Chequamegon National Forest provides the Northwoods setting
- Addresses the growing participation of women in distance Nordic skiing
- Classic technique only — honoring traditional skiing
- Benefits from the Birkebeiner trail grooming and infrastructure
