## Climate

Siberian continental

Extreme Siberian continental climate near Lake Baikal. March temperatures typically -20°C to -5°C. Lake Baikal moderates temperatures slightly near shore but inland cold is severe.

## History

The Baikal Ski Marathon takes place near Listvyanka on the shores of Lake Baikal, the world's deepest and oldest freshwater lake. The race offers a unique Siberian wilderness experience.

### Notable Facts

- Lake Baikal holds 20% of the world's unfrozen surface freshwater
- The lake is a UNESCO World Heritage Site
- One of the most remote Russialoppet events
- Siberian taiga setting with frozen lake panoramas
