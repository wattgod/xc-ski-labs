## Course

Trails at the base of Mount Washington through the White Mountain National Forest. Part of a six-area Nordic network. Rolling terrain with views of the Presidential Range.

Format: loop  
Surface: Machine-groomed trails through forest  
Technical rating: 3/5  
Grooming: Good machine grooming, multi-area network

## History

Great Glen Trails has hosted Nordic racing since the mid-1990s at the base of Mount Washington. The series includes multiple events through winter including the signature 'Ski & Shoe to the Clouds' on the Mount Washington Auto Road.

### Notable Facts

- Racing at the base of Mount Washington since ~1994
- Part of the White Mountain Nordic six-area network
- Signature event climbs 2,200 ft on the Auto Road
- Mix of timed races and fun community events
