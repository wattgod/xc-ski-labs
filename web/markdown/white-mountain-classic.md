## Course

Classic-only race through the Jackson XC trail network in the White Mountains of New Hampshire. The course weaves through birch and spruce forests with rolling terrain and scenic mountain views. Well-groomed trails with a mix of climbs and descents that reward good technique.

Format: loop  
Surface: Machine-groomed classic tracks through White Mountain forest  
Technical rating: 3/5  
Grooming: Well-maintained by Jackson XC ski touring foundation

### Features

- Scenic White Mountain views throughout the course
- Rolling forested terrain through birch and spruce
- Classic technique only — rewards traditional skiing skills
- Jackson XC has one of the most extensive trail networks in New England
- Post-race gathering in the charming village of Jackson

## Climate

New England mountain winter

Jackson NH in late January is typically cold and snowy. Temperatures range from -15°C to -5°C. The White Mountains receive reliable snowfall, though freeze-thaw cycles can affect trail conditions. Elevation provides reasonable snow security.

Typical race-day temperature: -15 to -5°C

Challenges:
- New England freeze-thaw cycles can create icy conditions
- Mountain weather can change rapidly
- Wind exposure on open sections
- Coastal storms can bring heavy wet snow

## History

The White Mountain Classic grew out of the Jackson XC ski touring community, one of the oldest and most respected Nordic trail networks in New England. The race celebrates classic technique skiing through the White Mountains, drawing primarily from the New England skiing community. Jackson has been a hub for Nordic skiing since the 1970s.

### Notable Facts

- Jackson XC operates one of the most extensive groomed trail networks in the northeastern US
- The White Mountains are a traditional stronghold of New England Nordic skiing
- Jackson village has a long history as a ski resort town dating to the early 20th century
- Classic technique only — one of the few remaining technique-pure races in New England
