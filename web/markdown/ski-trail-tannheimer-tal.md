30km course through the picturesque Tannheimer Tal, a high valley surrounded by dramatic Tirolean limestone peaks. The route follows the valley floor and lower slopes, offering both classic and skating disciplines. Excellent grooming on a scenic course that showcases Austrian mountain valley skiing at its best.

## Climate

Alpine valley

The Tannheimer Tal is a high valley at 1,100m in the Northern Limestone Alps. January conditions typically bring reliable snow cover with cold temperatures moderated by the valley's sheltered position. Inversions can trap cold air, ensuring excellent snow preservation.

Typical race-day temperature: -10 to -1°C

Challenges:
- Valley inversions can produce very cold morning starts
- Altitude at 1,100m adds physiological challenge for lowland skiers
- Sun exposure on south-facing valley sections can soften snow
- January daylight hours are limited

## History

The Ski Trail Tannheimer Tal has been held since 1985 in one of Tirol's most beautiful high valleys. The Tannheimer Tal is renowned as one of Austria's premier Nordic skiing destinations, with an extensive trail network and a deep commitment to cross-country skiing culture. The race has grown to attract over 2,000 participants from across Central Europe.

### Notable Facts

- Tannheimer Tal is one of Austria's most popular Nordic skiing valleys
- High valley at 1,100m ensures reliable snow conditions
- Attracts participants from Austria, Germany, and across Central Europe
- Both classic and skating disciplines reflect the valley's dual-discipline culture
