## History

The Kuzbass Ski Marathon takes place in the Kemerovo Oblast, the heart of Russia's Kuzbass coal mining region in western Siberia. Despite the industrial character of the region, the surrounding forests support a strong skiing community.

### Notable Facts

- Kuzbass is Russia's largest coal mining region
- Strong industrial working-class skiing tradition
- Part of the Russialoppet national marathon series
- Kemerovo Oblast has invested in Nordic skiing infrastructure
