## Course

A relatively flat multi-loop course based at the Planica Nordic Centre, venue of the 2023 FIS Nordic World Ski Championships. The terrain is gentle and beginner-friendly, making it accessible to recreational skiers. Set beneath the dramatic Julian Alps with views of the famous Planica ski jumping hills.

Format: multi-loop  
Surface: Machine-groomed tracks on World Championship-grade venue  
Technical rating: 2/5  
Grooming: World Championship venue standard — excellent machine grooming

## Climate

Alpine valley winter

Late January in the Planica valley at 850m altitude brings reliable cold and good snow cover. Temperatures range from -10°C to 0°C. The valley location beneath the Julian Alps provides some wind shelter. Snow is generally reliable thanks to the altitude and north-facing aspect.

Typical race-day temperature: -10 to 0°C

Challenges:
- January thaw risk at moderate 850m altitude
- Valley fog can settle in the Planica basin
- Wind channeling through the narrow valley
- Variable snow conditions between shaded and exposed sections

## History

The Planica Ski Marathon is one of the newest events on the European marathon circuit, launched in 2020 at the Planica Nordic Centre in Slovenia's Julian Alps. The venue gained global attention as host of the 2023 FIS Nordic World Ski Championships. Joining the Euroloppet series in 2025 marks a significant milestone, bringing international marathon skiers to a venue already renowned for ski jumping and World Cup cross-country racing.

### Notable Facts

- Founded in 2020 — one of the newest Euroloppet events
- Raced at the venue of the 2023 FIS Nordic World Ski Championships
- Planica is historically famous for ski jumping — first 100m+ jump in 1936
- Joined the Euroloppet series in 2025
- Slovenia's growing presence on the international marathon skiing calendar
- Flat course design makes it one of the most beginner-friendly Euroloppet events
