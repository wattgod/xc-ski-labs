## History

The Chama Chile Ski Classic is a small community ski race in Chama, New Mexico, one of the southernmost Nordic ski venues in the US. The high altitude and Southwestern character make it unique.

### Notable Facts

- One of the highest-altitude Nordic races in the US at ~2,400m
- Chama is a historic railroad town on the NM/CO border
- Green chile is a local tradition at the finish
