## History

StafettVasan was launched in 2003 as part of the Vasaloppet organization's effort to make the legendary Sälen-to-Mora course accessible to more skiers. By splitting the 90km course into five relay legs, teams of friends, families, and colleagues can collectively experience the full Vasaloppet route without each individual needing the fitness for 90km. The concept was an immediate hit, and StafettVasan has grown to become the world's largest ski relay with over 2,400 teams and 12,000 participants annually.

### Notable Facts

- World's largest ski relay with 2,400+ teams and 12,000+ participants
- Founded in 2003 alongside Nattvasan during Vasaloppet Winter Week expansion
- Makes the Vasaloppet course accessible to skiers who cannot race the full 90km
- Exchange zones at historic checkpoints — Smågan, Mångsbodarna, Risberg, Evertsberg, Oxberg
- The anchor leg skier crosses the same finish line as Vasaloppet champions
- Teams range from elite club squads to corporate and family groups
