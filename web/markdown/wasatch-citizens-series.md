## History

The Wasatch Citizens Series takes advantage of the Olympic-legacy infrastructure at Soldier Hollow, built for the 2002 Salt Lake City Winter Olympics. The venue continues to host national-level competitions alongside citizen races.

### Notable Facts

- Soldier Hollow hosted the 2002 Olympic cross-country and biathlon events
- Olympic-standard trail grooming and infrastructure remain
- Utah is bidding to host another Winter Olympics (2034)
- Wasatch Range receives an average of 12+ meters of snow annually
