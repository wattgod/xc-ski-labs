## History

The Teacup Classic is a small community ski race on the Teacup Lake Nordic trail system, a volunteer-maintained area on the eastern flank of Mt. Hood in Oregon.

### Notable Facts

- Teacup Lake trails are entirely volunteer-maintained
- Set on the eastern flank of Mt. Hood
- Part of Oregon's grassroots Nordic ski community
