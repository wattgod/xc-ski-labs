## History

Pogostan Hiihto is one of Finland's longest-established mass skiing events, dating back to approximately 1960. Organized by Ilomantsin Urheilijat, a local sports club in Ilomantsi, it has served the eastern Finnish skiing community for over six decades. The race takes place in Finland's easternmost municipality, near the Russian border, through authentic Karelian wilderness.

### Notable Facts

- One of Finland's oldest mass skiing events, dating to the 1960s
- Organized by Ilomantsin Urheilijat sports club
- Located in Finland's easternmost municipality (Ilomantsi)
- Offers both timed and untimed options for different skier levels
- Both classic and freestyle technique available
- 60km main distance through North Karelian wilderness
