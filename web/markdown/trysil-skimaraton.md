## Climate

Eastern Norwegian continental winter

Trysil's inland location near the Swedish border provides reliably cold and snowy winters. Temperatures typically range from -15°C to -3°C during the February-March race window. Continental climate ensures good snow conditions.

## History

Trysil Skimaraton has been a citizen ski race since approximately the 1980s, set in the forests surrounding Trysil, Norway's largest ski resort destination. The race is part of the Skiplukkern Langlopscup Innlandet, a regional long-distance ski cup in the Innlandet county of eastern Norway.
