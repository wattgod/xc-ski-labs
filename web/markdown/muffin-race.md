## Course

A 25km loop through the Bridger Mountains at Bohart Ranch Cross Country Ski Center. The course winds through subalpine forest with meadow openings offering views of the Bridger Range and Gallatin Valley. Moderate climbing with some fun descents.

Format: loop  
Surface: Machine-groomed Nordic trails at Bohart Ranch  
Technical rating: 2/5  
Grooming: Good quality grooming at Bohart Ranch

## Climate

Northern Rocky Mountain winter

Late January in the Bridger Mountains brings cold, dry conditions with reliable snowpack. Temperatures typically -15°C to -5°C. Altitude adds bite to the cold.

Typical race-day temperature: -15 to -5°C

Challenges:
- High altitude cold — temperatures can drop below -20°C
- Dry, powdery snow can make grooming challenging
- Wind exposure on open meadow sections

## History

A grassroots race organized by the Bridger Ski Foundation and held at Bohart Ranch near Bozeman. Known for its homemade muffin tradition at the finish line, it's a beloved community event that draws local skiers for a fun winter outing in the Bridger Mountains.

### Notable Facts

- Named for the homemade muffins served at the finish
- Bohart Ranch sits at 6,000+ feet in the Bridger Mountains
- One of Montana's most casual and community-driven Nordic events
- The Bozeman area has a thriving Nordic skiing community
