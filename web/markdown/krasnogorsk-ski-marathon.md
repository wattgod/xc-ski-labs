## History

The Krasnogorsk Ski Marathon takes place in Krasnogorsk, a city directly adjacent to Moscow's northwestern boundary. Its proximity to the capital makes it one of the most accessible Russialoppet events.

### Notable Facts

- One of the most accessible Russialoppet events by public transport
- Large field drawn from Moscow's metropolitan population
