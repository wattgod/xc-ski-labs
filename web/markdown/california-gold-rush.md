## Climate

Sierra Nevada high mountain spring

Late March at 7,000 feet in the Sierra Nevada brings spring skiing conditions. Temperatures range from -5°C to 10°C with strong sun. The Sierra snowpack is typically deep by late March, but warm spring conditions can soften trails by midday. UV exposure is intense.

Typical race-day temperature: -5 to 10°C

Challenges:
- Spring snow conditions — hard and fast in the morning, soft by afternoon
- Intense high-altitude UV exposure requires sun protection
- Sierra storms can still deliver significant snowfall in late March
- Altitude around 7,000 feet affects aerobic performance
- Wax selection critical as temperatures change rapidly through the race

## History

The California Gold Rush takes its name from the state's famous 1849 gold rush and serves as the season-ending finale of the American Ski Marathon Series. Held at Royal Gorge Cross Country Ski Resort — one of the largest XC ski areas in North America — the race offers a spectacular Sierra Nevada setting. The freestyle-only format attracts skating enthusiasts looking to close out the racing season on fast spring snow.

### Notable Facts

- American Ski Marathon Series season finale
- Held at Royal Gorge, one of North America's largest XC ski resorts
- Name references California's 1849 Gold Rush history
- Freestyle-only format — skating technique required
- Spring conditions often provide fast, firm morning snow
- Donner Pass area is steeped in Western frontier history
