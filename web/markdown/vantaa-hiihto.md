## History

The Vantaa-hiihto is a popular mass skiing event serving Finland's Helsinki metropolitan area, making long-distance Nordic skiing accessible to the capital region's large population. Part of Finland's rich massahiihto (mass skiing) culture, it provides residents of the greater Helsinki area with a marathon-distance ski experience without requiring travel to more remote venues. The event embodies the Finnish tradition that everyone should have access to long-distance skiing.

### Notable Facts

- Serves Finland's largest metropolitan population — Helsinki/Vantaa area
- Part of Finnish massahiihto (mass skiing) tradition
- Both classic and freestyle techniques offered
- One of the most accessible long-distance ski events in Finland
- Urban forest setting unique among Nordic ski marathons
- Multiple distance options from 30km to 50km
