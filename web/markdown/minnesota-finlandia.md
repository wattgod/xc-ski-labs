## Climate

Northern Minnesota deep continental winter

Mid-February in Bemidji is among the coldest racing environments in North America. Temperatures regularly drop below -20°C overnight and may not rise above -10°C on race day. The flat terrain around Lake Bemidji offers little wind protection. Reliable natural snow.

Typical race-day temperature: -25 to -8°C

Challenges:
- Among the coldest marathon ski venues in North America
- Frostbite risk is real — exposed skin freezes quickly
- Kick wax selection in extreme cold is critical
- Lake Bemidji winds can create severe wind chill
- Equipment management in sub-zero temperatures

## History

The Minnesota Finlandia was founded in 1982 to celebrate the deep Finnish heritage of northern Minnesota. Bemidji and the surrounding region were settled heavily by Finnish immigrants in the late 19th century, and the race honors that Nordic skiing tradition. As an original member of the American Ski Marathon Series, it helped establish citizen marathon skiing as a sport in the United States. The 'Nordic Festival of the North' branding reflects its role as both a race and a cultural celebration.
