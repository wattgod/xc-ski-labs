## Climate

Continental mountain winter

February in the Beskid Sądecki brings reliable cold and decent snow cover. The spa town's sheltered valley position moderates extremes. Temperatures typically -8°C to -1°C. The region receives consistent snowfall from December through March.

Typical race-day temperature: -8 to -1°C

Challenges:
- Variable snow conditions in lower spa town sections
- Fog can develop in valley sections during morning hours
- Temperature inversions trap cold air in sheltered valleys
- Late-season thaw risk at lower elevations

## History

Founded in 1985 as a tribute to Polish aviators, this race through the Beskid Sądecki hills has become a fixture of the Polish XC ski calendar. Krynica-Zdrój's status as Poland's premier mountain spa town gives the event a unique post-race recovery option. The race draws primarily from the southern Polish skiing community.

### Notable Facts

- Named in honor of Polish aviators — a regional memorial tradition
- Krynica-Zdrój is known as the 'Pearl of Polish Spas'
- The Beskid Sądecki range offers some of Poland's most reliable natural snow
- Post-race thermal spring soaks are a popular tradition among participants
- One of several citizen ski races in Poland's southern mountain belt
