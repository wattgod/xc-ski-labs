## Course

Loop course through the rolling terrain around St. Johann in Tirol, passing through meadows and forests with views of the Wilder Kaiser mountain range. The course features moderate climbs through the Kitzbuhel Alps foothills with well-groomed classic tracks.

## History

Founded in 1973 in the Tyrolean skiing heartland of St. Johann in Tirol. The Koasalauf takes its name from the Koasa (Wilder Kaiser) mountain range that dominates the skyline. The race grew as part of the Euroloppet series, establishing itself as Austria's premier long-distance classic event. The region has deep Nordic skiing roots, with well-maintained trail networks serving both competitive and recreational skiers.

### Notable Facts

- Named after the Koasa (Wilder Kaiser) mountain range visible throughout the course
- Member of the Euroloppet series
- St. Johann in Tirol is one of Austria's most important Nordic skiing centers
- The region hosted cross-country events during the 1964 and 1976 Winter Olympics nearby
- Offers a shorter 25km distance for recreational participants
