## Course

70km through the fell landscape surrounding Levi ski resort in Kittilä. The course traverses multiple fells with significant climbing, passing through birch forest and above-treeline tundra with panoramic Lapland views.

Format: loop  
Surface: Machine-groomed tracks, resort-quality near Levi  
Technical rating: 3/5

## Climate

Arctic continental

Levi sits in Finnish Lapland at 67°N, just above the Arctic Circle. Reliable snow from November to May. April racing offers returning sunlight and milder temperatures, though exposed fell terrain can be bitterly cold and windy.

Typical race-day temperature: -12 to -2°C

Challenges:
- Wind exposure on open fell terrain
- Long distance in cold conditions
- Variable snow conditions between sheltered valleys and wind-scoured fells
- Late-season sun can create crusty surface conditions

## History

The Levi Ski Marathon is held in the fell landscape around Levi, Finland's largest ski resort, in the municipality of Kittilä. The 70km course takes skiers across multiple fells in the heart of Finnish Lapland. Levi is famous as a World Cup alpine skiing venue, and the ski marathon leverages its extensive trail infrastructure.

### Notable Facts

- Based at Levi, Finland's largest and most visited ski resort
- Kittilä municipality is one of Finland's largest by area
- Levi hosts annual FIS Alpine Ski World Cup events
- The surrounding fells reach up to 531m (Levi fell summit)
- Finnish Lapland's long snow season allows racing well into April
