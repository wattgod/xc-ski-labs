January in Lääne-Virumaa brings the coldest and most reliable snow conditions of the Estonian winter. Temperatures typically -12°C to -4°C. January timing maximizes snow probability in the Baltic climate, though thaw events are still possible.

## History

The Neeruti Maraton is a skate-technique event in the Estoloppet series, held in the Neeruti landscape reserve of Lääne-Virumaa. The reserve's drumlin formations — elongated glacial hills — provide unusual terrain for the Baltic region and give the course more character than Estonia's typically flat landscape.

### Notable Facts

- Part of the Estoloppet series — Estonia's national ski marathon circuit
- Neeruti landscape reserve features drumlins — rare glacial landforms
- Skate technique only — one of the Estoloppet series' specialty format events
- Lääne-Virumaa is one of Estonia's northernmost counties
- Drumlins were formed during the last Ice Age by advancing glaciers
- January timing targets the most reliable snow window in the Baltic
