Sjusjoen sits at 800-900m ASL in Innlandet, providing reliable winter conditions from December through April. Race day temperatures typically range from -15°C to -3°C in the January-February timeframe.

## History

The Sjusjoen Skimarathon emerged in the 1990s on one of Norway's most extensive and well-maintained cross-country skiing trail networks. Sjusjoen has been a premier Norwegian skiing destination for decades, and the marathon leverages its world-class infrastructure. The race serves as an important seeding event for both Birkebeinerrennet and Vasaloppet.
