## Course

A 42km classic race through the Tierra Mayor valley near Ushuaia. The course traverses sub-Antarctic lenga beech forests and open valleys with views of the Martial Glacier and surrounding Fuegian Andes. The terrain is rolling with moderate climbs. Wind exposure on open sections is the defining challenge. This is a separate event from the Ushuaia Loppet, held later in the season.

Format: out-and-back  
Surface: Groomed classic tracks through sub-Antarctic terrain; conditions variable  
Technical rating: 3/5  
Grooming: Basic grooming — limited equipment in remote Patagonian location

## Climate

Sub-Antarctic maritime winter

September is late winter in the Southern Hemisphere. Ushuaia at 55°S brings maritime sub-Antarctic conditions with fierce winds, rain-snow transitions, and limited daylight. Temperatures typically -3°C to 5°C. The maritime climate means snow conditions are variable and challenging — freeze-thaw cycles are common.

Typical race-day temperature: -3 to 5°C

Challenges:
- Fierce Patagonian winds — gusts can exceed 80 km/h
- Rain-snow transitions due to maritime climate
- Limited September daylight at 55°S latitude
- Variable and often icy snow conditions
- Remote location with limited winter infrastructure

## History

The Ushuaia Ski Marathon is a separate event from the better-known Ushuaia Loppet, held later in the Tierra del Fuego winter season. Set in the Tierra Mayor valley near the world's southernmost city, it offers a marathon-distance classic race in one of the planet's most remote and dramatic landscapes. The event is part of Ushuaia's growing winter sports calendar, which markets the city's position as the gateway to Antarctica.
