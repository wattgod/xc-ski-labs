## Climate

Alpine South Tyrolean winter

The Gsies Valley at 1,300 meters altitude is one of the most snow-reliable valleys in the Alps. Temperatures range from -12°C to 2°C in late February. The high, enclosed valley position traps cold air and preserves snow quality. The race has never been cancelled due to lack of snow since its founding in 1984.

Typical race-day temperature: -10 to 0°C

Challenges:

- Valley winds can funnel through narrow sections

## History

Founded in 1984, the Gsieser Tal Lauf has built its reputation on one extraordinary fact: it has never been cancelled. The Gsies Valley's position at 1,300 meters in the eastern Dolomites provides some of the most reliable natural snow conditions in the Alps. The race takes place in a German-speaking valley in South Tyrol, where Alpine farming traditions meet Italian flair. As a Euroloppet member, it draws participants from across central Europe seeking guaranteed snow and stunning mountain scenery.

### Notable Facts

- Never been cancelled since its founding in 1984
- Set in the Gsies Valley (Val Casies) at 1,300 meters altitude
- Euroloppet member event
- South Tyrol's bilingual German-Italian culture adds unique character
- The valley is one of the most snow-reliable locations in the entire Alps
- Traditional Tyrolean hospitality at feed stations and finish area
