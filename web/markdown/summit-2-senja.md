## Course

A demanding 60km point-to-point from Bardufoss to Finnsnes, crossing three municipalities (Malselv, Sorreisa, and Senja). The course features everything from long flat stretches with spectacular fjord views to the iconic Magdaskaret climb. Finishes in the city center of Finnsnes. Mountains to fjords — one of the most dramatic courses on the Ski Classics calendar.

Format: point-to-point  
Surface: Groomed trails through Arctic mountain and fjord terrain

## History

Summit 2 Senja is the Ski Classics Pro Tour Grand Finale, first held in 2024 as the season-ending showpiece event. The 60km course from Bardufoss to Finnsnes takes athletes from the mountains to the fjords across three Arctic municipalities, featuring the iconic Magdaskaret climb. Voted 'Best Event of the Year' twice by Pro Team athletes and staff, it has rapidly established itself as one of the most dramatic and celebrated events on the entire Ski Classics calendar. Up to 250 recreational spots are available for qualified skiers.
