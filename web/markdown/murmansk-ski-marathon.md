## History

The Murmansk Ski Marathon is part of the Prazdnik Severa (Celebration of the North), a historic Polar Olympiad tradition that dates back decades. The ski marathon component was formalized in 1974. The race has been a member of the Russialoppet series and formerly held Euroloppet membership. Located on the Kola Peninsula above the Arctic Circle, it represents one of the most extreme latitudes for organized marathon ski racing.

### Notable Facts

- Part of the historic Prazdnik Severa (Celebration of the North / Polar Olympiad)
- Founded in 1974 as a formal marathon distance
- One of the northernmost major ski marathons in the world (69°N)
- Member of the Russialoppet series
- Formerly held Euroloppet membership
- Both classic and freestyle distances offered on separate days
