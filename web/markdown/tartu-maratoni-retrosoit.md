## Course

A 30km classic course through the rolling hills and forests around Otepää, following a shortened version of the main Tartu Marathon route. The retro format requires participants to use traditional wooden skis and period-appropriate equipment, adding a heritage dimension to the skiing experience.

Format: point-to-point  
Surface: Groomed classic tracks through forest and open farmland  
Technical rating: 2/5  
Grooming: Standard grooming adapted for the retro format with wider tracks

### Features

- Mandatory wooden skis and traditional equipment
- Course through the hilly Otepää winter sports region
- Heritage celebration of Estonian skiing traditions
- Part of the broader Tartu Marathon festival weekend
- Period costumes encouraged among participants

## History

Launched in 2005 as a companion event to the main Tartu Marathon, the Retrosõit celebrates Estonia's skiing heritage by requiring participants to use traditional wooden skis. The event is a nostalgic tribute to the early days of Nordic skiing in Estonia, when the Tartu Marathon was first held in 1960. The 30km distance is deliberately shorter to accommodate the slower wooden equipment.

### Notable Facts

- Participants must use traditional wooden skis with leather bindings
- Period costumes and vintage equipment are encouraged and celebrated
- Companion event to the main Tartu Marathon (Worldloppet member since 1983)
- Otepää is known as the 'Winter Capital of Estonia'
- The main Tartu Marathon has been held since 1960
