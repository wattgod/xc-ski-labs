Point-to-point from Mancelona to Bellaire through northern Michigan's forests and rolling terrain. The course follows a mix of old railroad grades and forest trails with moderate elevation changes. Classic northern hardwood forest scenery throughout.

## Climate

Northern Michigan lake-effect winter

Early February in northern Michigan delivers cold, snowy conditions enhanced by Lake Michigan lake-effect snow. Temperatures typically range from -15°C to -3°C. The region receives abundant snowfall, though recent warm winters have occasionally threatened conditions.

Typical race-day temperature: -15 to -3°C

## History

The White Pine Stampede has been northern Michigan's premier Nordic ski marathon since 1977. As the state's oldest point-to-point XC race, it holds a special place in the Midwest skiing community. The race takes its name from the white pine forests that once dominated northern Michigan before the logging era. For nearly five decades, it has drawn skiers to the quiet beauty of Michigan's northern Lower Peninsula.

### Notable Facts

- Northern Michigan's oldest point-to-point Nordic marathon since 1977
- Named for the white pine forests of northern Michigan's logging heritage
- Point-to-point format from Mancelona to Bellaire
- Strong volunteer tradition — local community organizes the entire event
- Offers both classic and freestyle in multiple distances
- Part of the Michigan Cup racing series
