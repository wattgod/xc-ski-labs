## Course

Loop course circling Furusjøen lake in the foothills of the Rondane mountain range. The circuit follows classic tracks through forest and open mountain terrain, with views toward the Rondane peaks. Rolling terrain with moderate climbs as the course traces the lake's perimeter.

## Climate

Norwegian mountain plateau winter

February at 900m altitude in the Rondane foothills means deep winter. Cold, stable conditions are typical. Temperatures generally -15°C to -5°C. Snow is highly reliable at this altitude and time of year. Short daylight hours in early February.

Typical race-day temperature: -15 to -5°C

Challenges:
- Deep cold in February at mountain altitude
- Wind exposure on open sections around the lake
- Short February daylight — early start required
- Mountain weather can change rapidly

## History

A 45km classic race circling Furusjøen lake in Norway's Innlandet region, in the foothills of the Rondane mountain range. Part of the Ski Classics Challengers series, it also functions as a seeding race for the prestigious Birkebeinerrennet. The race attracts around 500 participants, many of whom are training and qualifying for the Birken.

### Notable Facts

- Functions as a Birkebeinerrennet seeding/qualifying race
- Located in the foothills of Rondane, one of Norway's oldest national parks
- Part of the Ski Classics Challengers series
- Loop format around Furusjøen lake is unusual for Norwegian ski marathons
- ~517 participants reflects its role as a qualifier rather than destination race
