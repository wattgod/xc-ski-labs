## History

The Svalbard Ski Marathon has been held since 1993 in Longyearbyen, the main settlement on the Svalbard archipelago at 78°N latitude. It is the world's northernmost touring race and the largest annual event on Svalbard, complete with armed polar bear guards patrolling the course.

### Notable Facts

- World's northernmost touring race (78°N latitude)
- Armed polar bear guards patrol the course
- Founded 1993 — largest annual event on Svalbard
- 24-hour daylight during the late April/May race date
- Classic technique through treeless Arctic wilderness
