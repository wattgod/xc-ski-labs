Loop course through the Sant'Orso meadows and surrounding valleys beneath the Gran Paradiso massif in Italy's oldest national park. Stunning Dolomite-level scenery with moderate climbing.

## History

Founded in 1975, the Marcia Gran Paradiso is one of Italy's historic ski marathons set in the spectacular Cogne valley beneath the Gran Paradiso massif. The race takes place in Italy's oldest national park and has become part of the Ski Classics Challengers circuit.

### Notable Facts

- Set in Italy's oldest national park (Gran Paradiso, established 1922)
- Cogne is one of Italy's premier cross-country ski destinations
- Part of the Ski Classics Challengers circuit
- The Gran Paradiso massif (4,061m) is the only mountain over 4,000m entirely within Italy
