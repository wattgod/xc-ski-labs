## Climate

Canadian Rocky Mountain winter

Lake Louise sits at 1,540m in the Canadian Rockies with reliable cold winter conditions. March temperatures typically range from -15°C to -5°C. Chinook winds can occasionally bring rapid warming, but the altitude usually guarantees excellent snow.

Typical race-day temperature: -15 to -5°C

Challenges:
- High altitude at 1,540m can affect effort
- Cold Rocky Mountain temperatures in early March
- Occasional Chinook wind events can warm conditions rapidly
- Remote mountain setting requires advance planning

## History

The Lake Louise Loppet was founded in 1972, making it the longest-running loppet in Western Canada. Organized by the Calgary Ski Club, it has been a community institution for over five decades. The event celebrated its 50th anniversary in 2022. Set against the spectacular backdrop of the Canadian Rockies in Banff National Park, it emphasizes participation and community over competition.
