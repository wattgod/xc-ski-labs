## Course

Loop course through the Jizera Mountains starting and finishing in Bedřichov. The course winds through dense spruce forest with rolling terrain and several significant climbs. The mountains were once heavily logged and have been reforested, creating atmospheric dense forest skiing.

Format: loop

## History

Founded in 1968, Jizerská 50 has grown from a regional Czech event to one of the premier races on the international long-distance skiing calendar. Set in the Jizera Mountains near the Polish border, the race draws nearly 10,000 participants. It became a Worldloppet member and later joined the Ski Classics Grand Classic series, cementing its status as a world-class event. The Jizera Mountains have a long skiing tradition dating back to the 19th century.

### Notable Facts

- Founded in 1968 during the Prague Spring — a period of political liberalization in Czechoslovakia
- The Jizera Mountains have hosted competitive skiing since the late 1800s
- Part of both Worldloppet and Ski Classics Grand Classic series
- Nearby Liberec hosted the 2009 FIS Nordic World Ski Championships
- The race week includes events for all abilities across multiple distances
- Czech Republic's most important long-distance ski race
