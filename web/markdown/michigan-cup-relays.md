## Course

Relay-format racing on groomed Nordic trail systems across Michigan. Each leg is approximately 10km on courses that range from flat to gently rolling, depending on the host venue. The relay format creates an electric team atmosphere unlike individual racing.

## History

The Michigan Cup has been the backbone of competitive cross-country skiing in Michigan since 1975. The series rotates through venues across the state, building rivalries between clubs like Cross Country Ski Headquarters, Nordic Rocks, and Team NordicSkiRacer. It has been instrumental in developing Michigan's Nordic ski racing community.

### Notable Facts

- One of the longest-running club relay series in the United States
- Rotating venues include Cross Country Ski Headquarters, Hanson Hills, and Forbush Corner
- The series has been a feeder system for Michigan's competitive skiers for decades
- Club rivalries drive participation and community engagement
