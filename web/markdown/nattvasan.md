## Course

The same iconic 90km Vasaloppet course from Sälen to Mora, but skied in free technique through the Swedish night. Two-person teams must stay within 20 minutes of each other. The same seven checkpoints provide warmth and refueling. The darkness transforms familiar terrain into an entirely different challenge.

## History

Nattvasan was founded in 2003 as part of the expanding Vasaloppet Winter Week program. The concept was simple but radical: ski the entire Vasaloppet course through the night using headlamps. The 90km distance is raced as two-person teams in free technique (skate), while a 45km solo option exists. The event quickly captured the imagination of the Swedish skiing community, growing to over 4,000 participants. It has become one of the most iconic night races in any sport, turning the familiar Dalarna landscape into an otherworldly experience.

### Notable Facts
