## Course

Point-to-point from Nordli in Norway's Trøndelag region across the Scandinavian mountain border to Gäddede in Swedish Jämtland. The route follows the historic escape path used by Norwegian refugees fleeing to Sweden during WWII. Terrain mixes forested valleys with exposed mountain crossings at the border.

## History

Founded in 1950 to commemorate the flight of Norwegian refugees across the mountains to neutral Sweden during the German occupation in 1944. The 44km race follows the actual escape route from Nordli to Gäddede. It is one of Scandinavia's most historically significant ski races, carrying deep meaning for border communities. Part of the Ski Classics Challengers series.

### Notable Facts

- Commemorates the 1944 refugee flight from Nazi-occupied Norway to Sweden
- Founded just 5 years after the war's end, in 1950
- The only major ski marathon that crosses an international border
- Race name means 'The Refugee Race' in Norwegian
- Part of the Ski Classics Challengers series
- Start in Norway, finish in Sweden — two countries, one race
