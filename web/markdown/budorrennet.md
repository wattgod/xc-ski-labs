## History

Budorrennet has been a Norwegian turrenn since approximately the 1970s, held on the Hedmarksvidda plateau in Innlandet county. Its flat profile has made it a popular choice for recreational skiers looking to complete their first marathon-distance ski race. The race is part of the Skiplukkern Langlopscup Innlandet, the regional long-distance ski cup.
