Late January in Dalarna's interior means deep cold and reliable snow cover. Temperatures range from -20°C to -5°C. ## History

The Orsa Gronklitt Ski Marathon takes place at one of Sweden's most respected Nordic ski venues in the heart of Dalarna — the spiritual home of Swedish skiing and the Vasaloppet. The race serves as an official qualifying event for both Vasaloppet and Tjejvasan, drawing skiers who use it as preparation for the larger events in the same region. Gronklitt's extensive trail network and reliable conditions make it a natural host for citizen racing.

### Notable Facts
