## Course

Multi-loop course at Giants Ridge Recreation Area, a former World Cup venue. The terrain is demanding with constant rolling hills, punchy climbs, and fast technical descents through mixed forest. World Cup-standard grooming on purpose-built trails.

Format: loop  
Surface: Machine-groomed World Cup-standard trails with classic tracks and skating lanes  
Technical rating: 4/5  
Grooming: World Cup venue grooming standards — among the best trail surfaces in North America

### Features

- Giants Ridge hosted FIS World Cup events
- Relentless rolling terrain with no flat recovery sections
- Both classic and freestyle distances offered
- Technical descents require strong downhill skills
- Iron Range mining country setting with boreal forest

## Climate

Northern Minnesota continental winter

Late February on the Iron Range delivers consistent cold and reliable snow. Temperatures range from -15°C to -2°C. The area's inland location and northern latitude provide excellent snow preservation. Wind exposure varies by course section.

Typical race-day temperature: -15 to -2°C

Challenges:
- Iron Range cold snaps can push temps well below -20°C
- Exposed ridgeline sections with wind chill
- Variable wax conditions through the day as temps shift
- Altitude is modest but hilly terrain is relentless

## History

The Pepsi Challenge has been a fixture of Minnesota Nordic racing since 1987, leveraging the world-class trail infrastructure at Giants Ridge. The venue's FIS World Cup hosting credentials give the race a pedigree unmatched by most citizen races in North America. Set in the Iron Range — the historic mining region of northern Minnesota — the race draws a deep competitive field from the Midwest's vibrant Nordic community.

### Notable Facts

- Giants Ridge hosted FIS Cross-Country World Cup events
- Set in Minnesota's Iron Range, the historic mining heartland
- One of the few American citizen races on a World Cup venue
- Offers both classic and freestyle 50km options
- Part of the American Ski Marathon Series
