## Course

Point-to-point through Dalarna wilderness, rolling terrain with no major climbs but relentless undulation over 90km. The final 10km into Mora are fast and flat.

Format: point-to-point  
Surface: Machine-groomed classic tracks through forest, marsh, and lake terrain  
Technical rating: 3/5

## History

Founded in 1922 by newspaper editor Anders Pers to commemorate Gustav Vasa's 1520 flight from Danish soldiers on skis from Mora to Sälen. The race reverses his route. It has grown from 119 starters in 1922 to over 16,000, becoming the world's oldest and largest cross-country ski race. The Vasaloppet week now includes multiple events attracting 60,000+ participants across all distances.

### Notable Facts

- Named after King Gustav Vasa, who fled on skis in 1520 to rally Swedish independence
- First edition in 1922 had 119 starters — now exceeds 16,000
- Nils 'Mora-Nisse' Karlsson won 9 times between 1943-1954
- The race was cancelled only during WWII years (1940, 1942-1945)
- Finishing motto: 'I fäders spår, för framtids segrar' (In the footsteps of our fathers, for future victories)
- Part of both Worldloppet and Ski Classics series
