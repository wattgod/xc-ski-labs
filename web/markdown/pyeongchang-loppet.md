## Climate

East Asian continental mountain winter

February in PyeongChang brings cold, dry conditions influenced by Siberian air masses. The Taebaek Mountains receive reliable snowfall, supplemented by snowmaking infrastructure built for the 2018 Olympics. Typically -15°C to -5°C. Cold but generally sunny with low humidity.

Typical race-day temperature: -15 to -5°C

Challenges:
- Extreme cold from Siberian high-pressure systems
- Low humidity can affect breathing and hydration
- Wind exposure on the open Alpensia plateau
- Jet lag for European and American participants
