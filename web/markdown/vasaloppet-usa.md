## History

Founded in 1973 by a group of Scandinavian-Americans in Mora, Minnesota — a town named after the Swedish finish city of the original Vasaloppet. The race was created to honor Nordic ski heritage in the Upper Midwest, where generations of Swedish and Norwegian immigrants settled. It has grown into the largest cross-country ski race in the United States, attracting around 3,000 participants across all distances.

### Notable Facts

- Mora, Minnesota was founded by Swedish immigrants and named after the Vasaloppet finish town
- Largest cross-country ski race in the United States
- Licensed by the Swedish Vasaloppet organization
- Celebrates the deep Scandinavian-American heritage of Minnesota
- Originally 55km, adjusted over the years to current 58km course
