## Course

Point-to-point mountain course that climbs from forested valleys to the open fjäll plateau above treeline. Extended exposed sections across the windswept mountain top before descending back to sheltered forest. One of Sweden's most dramatic Nordic race courses.

Format: point-to-point  
Surface: Machine-groomed and wind-packed mountain trails  
Technical rating: 4/5  
Grooming: Groomed where possible; above-treeline sections rely on wind-packed and mechanically prepared snow

### Features

- Extended above-treeline skiing on the open fjäll plateau
- Dramatic transition from boreal forest to Arctic tundra landscape
- Mountain-top panoramic views across the Härjedalen mountain range
- One of the highest-altitude Nordic races in Scandinavia

## Climate

Scandinavian mountain (fjäll) climate

The Swedish fjäll delivers harsh, exposed mountain conditions. Race day temperatures range from -15°C to -3°C, but wind chill on the open plateau can make it feel far colder. Above treeline, wind speeds of 40-60 km/h are not uncommon. Late March brings longer daylight but the mountain plateau remains deeply frozen.

Typical race-day temperature: -12 to -3°C

Challenges:
- Extended above-treeline sections with severe wind exposure
- Whiteout conditions possible on the open fjäll plateau
- Temperature drop of 5-10°C between forest and mountain sections
- Late-season sun combined with high altitude increases UV risk

## History

Founded in 1982, Fjälltopploppet was created to showcase the unique above-treeline skiing terrain of the Swedish fjäll. Bruksvallarna has been a training base for Swedish national ski teams for decades, and the race takes advantage of the extensive trail network and mountain infrastructure. The course's passage across the open fjäll makes it one of the most exposed and dramatic Nordic races in Scandinavia.

### Notable Facts

- Bruksvallarna is a regular Swedish national team training venue
- The course crosses the open fjäll plateau above 900 meters
- The race has been shortened or rerouted in extreme wind years
- Härjedalen is one of Sweden's least populated regions with vast wilderness
