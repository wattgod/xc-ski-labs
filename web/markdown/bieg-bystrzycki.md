## History

Founded in 1992 shortly after the fall of communism in Poland, the Bieg Bystrzycki emerged from the strong Silesian cross-country skiing tradition. Bystrzyca Kłodzka, a historic town in the Kłodzko Valley, provided a natural setting for a mountain-oriented Nordic race. The event has remained a beloved regional fixture, drawing skiers from across southwestern Poland to the Sudeten mountains.

### Notable Facts

- Founded in 1992, one of the first post-communist era Polish ski races
- Bystrzyca Kłodzka is a historic fortified town dating to the 13th century
- The Sudeten mountains straddle the Polish-Czech border
- Part of the Lower Silesian regional ski race circuit
- The Kłodzko Valley is one of Poland's most scenic winter landscapes
