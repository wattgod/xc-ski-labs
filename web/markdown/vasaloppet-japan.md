## Course

A 45km skate race on a 15km loop course completed three times in Asahikawa, Hokkaido. The course winds through Hokkaido's snow-covered forests and open terrain. Named after the Swedish Vasaloppet, the Japanese edition adapts the long-distance skiing tradition to Hokkaido's extraordinary snow conditions. The loop format provides spectator-friendly racing with multiple pass-throughs of the start/finish area.

## History

Founded in 1981, Vasaloppet Japan brought the Scandinavian long-distance skiing tradition to Hokkaido. Named with permission from the original Swedish Vasaloppet, the race established itself in Asahikawa, one of Japan's coldest and snowiest cities. The event has grown over four decades to attract around 2,000 participants and serves as Japan's premier cross-country ski marathon. Hokkaido's extraordinary snow reliability — fed by Siberian air masses crossing the Sea of Japan — makes it one of the most snow-sure races in the world.
