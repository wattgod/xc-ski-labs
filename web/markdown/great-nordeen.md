## Course

A 30km point-to-point freestyle race from Mt. Bachelor to Wanoga Sno Park on Deschutes National Forest trails. Net downhill with rolling terrain through ponderosa pine and high-desert Cascade forest. Named in honor of local ski pioneer Emil Nordeen. Also includes a 15km fat bike race.

## Climate

Pacific Northwest volcanic mountain winter

February at Mt. Bachelor means abundant Cascade snow and moderate temperatures. The start at ~6,400 feet (1,950m) on the volcano's flanks ensures deep snow cover. Temperatures range from -10°C to 5°C. Pacific moisture delivers reliable snowfall, though it can be wetter and heavier than intermountain powder.

Typical race-day temperature: -10 to 5°C

Challenges:
- Pacific Northwest wet snow — heavier than intermountain powder
- Altitude at the start (~1,950m) — moderate performance impact
- Weather can shift rapidly on Mt. Bachelor's exposed flanks
- Point-to-point format means one-way logistics

## History

The Great Nordeen is Central Oregon's largest cross-country ski race, first held in 2004 and named in honor of local ski pioneer Emil Nordeen. The point-to-point course from Mt. Bachelor to Wanoga Sno Park runs through the Deschutes National Forest, leveraging Bend's exceptional Nordic skiing infrastructure. The event is organized by MBSEF (Mt. Bachelor Sports Education Foundation) and also includes a fat bike race, reflecting the multi-sport winter culture of Bend.
