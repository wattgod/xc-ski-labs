## Course

A 42km skate race through the Forêt du Massacre (Forest of Massacre) in the French Jura. The course traverses dense conifer forest and open ridge lines at around 1200m altitude, with meaningful climbs and technical descents. The forest takes its name from historical events and creates a dramatic, atmospheric course. Technical terrain through the forest rewards confident downhill skating and strong climbing.

Format: point-to-point  
Surface: Machine-groomed skate lanes through Jura mountain terrain

## Climate

Jura mountain winter

March in the Jura Mountains brings variable conditions. The 1200m altitude provides good snow depth, but the Jura's position between continental and Atlantic influences creates unpredictable weather. Typically -5°C to 5°C. Late-season warmth can affect snow conditions at lower elevations.

## History

Founded in 1975, the Traversée du Massacre is approaching its 50th edition, making it one of France's oldest and most storied ski marathons. The race crosses the Forêt du Massacre, a dense Jura forest whose name dates to a 16th-century military ambush. Based in Prémanon in the French Jura — a region that has produced many of France's top Nordic skiers — the race is a pillar of French cross-country skiing culture. The Jura Mountains remain France's heartland for Nordic skiing.
