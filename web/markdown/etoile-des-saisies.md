## Climate

High Alpine plateau winter

Late January at Les Saisies at 1,650m altitude brings reliable high-altitude winter conditions. Temperatures typically -10°C to 0°C. Les Saisies is known as one of the snowiest Nordic venues in the French Alps, with its north-facing position ensuring excellent snow retention.

Typical race-day temperature: -10 to 0°C

Challenges:
- High altitude at 1,650m — noticeable for lowland skiers
- Exposed plateau sections with wind chill
- Variable conditions between sheltered forest and open terrain
- Access roads can be challenging after heavy snowfall

## History

The Étoile des Saisies is based at Les Saisies, France's most prestigious Nordic ski resort. Les Saisies hosted the biathlon and Nordic combined events of the 1992 Albertville Winter Olympics, cementing its status as France's premier cross-country skiing venue. Part of the Marathon Ski Tour series. The resort sits at 1,650m with panoramic views of Mont Blanc and 120km of groomed Nordic trails.
