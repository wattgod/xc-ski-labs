## Climate

Pre-Alpine plateau winter

The Vercors plateau at 1,050m elevation delivers cold, snowy winters sheltered by surrounding limestone cliffs. January temperatures range from -10°C to 3°C. Snow coverage is generally reliable thanks to altitude and north-facing aspects, though the southern Alpine location can bring occasional warm spells.

Typical race-day temperature: -10 to 3°C

Challenges:
- Vercors wind exposure on open plateau sections
- Occasional warm Mediterranean influence can soften snow
- Altitude variation creates different snow conditions across the course
- January date means short daylight hours

## History

Founded in 1979, La Foulée Blanche is France's largest and most prestigious cross-country ski marathon. The race is held in Autrans on the Vercors plateau, which hosted the Nordic skiing events of the 1968 Grenoble Winter Olympics. The event has grown to attract approximately 2,000 participants, making it the premier event on the French citizen ski racing calendar. The Vercors — a dramatic limestone massif — provides a uniquely scenic setting among European marathon ski races.
