## Climate

Maritime subarctic

Sakhalin Island receives heavy snowfall from the Sea of Japan and Sea of Okhotsk. February temperatures typically -15°C to -5°C. Maritime influence creates wet, heavy snow.

Typical race-day temperature: -15 to -5°C

Challenges:
- Heavy maritime snowfall can disrupt conditions
- Sea of Okhotsk winds on exposed sections
- Wet snow creating slow conditions
- Remote island location — logistically challenging

## History

The Sakhalin Ski Marathon takes place on Russia's largest island in the Pacific. Sakhalin, once jointly administered with Japan, has a strong skiing tradition supported by heavy maritime snowfall.

### Notable Facts

- Sakhalin Island lies between the Sea of Japan and the Sea of Okhotsk
- Former Japanese territory with shared cultural heritage
- One of Russia's most remote Russialoppet events
- Heavy maritime snowfall creates deep, reliable snowpack
