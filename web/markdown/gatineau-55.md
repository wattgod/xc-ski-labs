## Course

Loop course through Gatineau Park on the NCC (National Capital Commission) trail network. The course traverses the Gatineau Hills with a mix of forested sections, ridgeline running, and valley crossings. Significant cumulative elevation gain across 55km makes this one of the hillier loppets in eastern Canada. Well-maintained NCC trails offer excellent classic skiing.

## History

The Gatineau 55 (also known as the Gatineau Loppet) has been a fixture of eastern Canadian skiing since 1979. Held in Gatineau Park just across the river from Ottawa, the race takes advantage of the National Capital Commission's extensive trail network. The bilingual character of the event — conducted in both English and French — reflects the unique cultural identity of the Ottawa-Gatineau region.

### Notable Facts

- Gatineau Park trails are maintained by the National Capital Commission (NCC)
- The park is a UNESCO-designated Biosphere Reserve candidate area
- One of the few bilingual (English/French) ski races in the world
- Proximity to Ottawa makes it one of the most accessible loppets in Canada
- The Gatineau Hills reach over 400m — modest but producing genuine climbing
- The race draws from both the Ontario and Quebec skiing communities
