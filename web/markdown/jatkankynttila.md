## History

The Jatkankynttila Ski Marathon is a Lapland mass skiing event based in Rovaniemi, the capital of Finnish Lapland at the Arctic Circle. Named after the iconic Jatkankynttila (Lumberjack's Candle) bridge — a modern landmark that has become a symbol of the city — the race takes advantage of Lapland's guaranteed March snow conditions. It represents the deep Finnish massahiihto tradition extended to the Arctic north.

### Notable Facts

- Named after the Jatkankynttila (Lumberjack's Candle) bridge in Rovaniemi
- Arctic Circle location — among the northernmost ski marathons in the world
- Rovaniemi is the official capital of Finnish Lapland
- Reliable March snow conditions guaranteed by Arctic latitude
- Part of Finnish massahiihto (mass skiing) culture
- Both classic and freestyle techniques offered
