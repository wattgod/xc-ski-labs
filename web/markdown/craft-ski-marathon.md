## History

The Craft Ski Marathon emerged in the early 2000s at Gronklitt, one of Sweden's most established cross-country skiing destinations near Orsa in Dalarna. It serves as the season opener for many Swedish recreational long-distance skiers and is part of both the Ski Classics Challengers circuit and the Svenska Langlopscupen 'The Originals' series.
