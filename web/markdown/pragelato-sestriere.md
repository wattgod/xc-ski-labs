## Course

A 30km point-to-point course from the Pragelato Nordic stadium (2006 Olympic cross-country venue) climbing to the alpine resort of Sestriere. The route features sustained climbing through Olympic-grade terrain with a dramatic high-altitude finish. The course uses sections of the original 2006 Turin Winter Olympics cross-country tracks.

Format: point-to-point  
Surface: Machine-groomed tracks on Olympic-standard trails with alpine terrain  
Technical rating: 3/5  
Grooming: High-quality grooming on Olympic-legacy infrastructure

### Features

- Start at the 2006 Turin Winter Olympics Nordic venue
- Finish at the iconic Sestriere alpine resort
- Sustained climbing on Olympic-grade trails
- High-altitude skiing above 2000m at the finish
- Views of the Cottian Alps and Monte Viso

## Climate

High alpine Piedmont winter

Early March at high altitude in the Piedmont Alps. The course climbs from 1520m to over 2000m, ensuring reliable snow cover well into spring. Temperatures typically -8 to 0 degrees Celsius at the start, colder at the summit finish. The exposed high-altitude sections can produce strong winds.

Typical race-day temperature: -8 to 0°C

Challenges:
- Significant altitude gain from start to finish
- Exposed high-altitude sections above 2000m with wind
- Spring sun can soften south-facing sections
- Altitude effects on breathing at the summit finish

## History

Founded in 2008, two years after the 2006 Turin Winter Olympics brought world-class Nordic infrastructure to Pragelato. The race was created to make use of the Olympic cross-country venue and connect it to the famous alpine resort of Sestriere, which hosted Olympic alpine events. The point-to-point format from the Nordic stadium to the alpine summit creates a unique Olympic-legacy experience.

### Notable Facts

- Pragelato hosted all cross-country skiing events at the 2006 Turin Winter Olympics
- Sestriere hosted Olympic alpine events including giant slalom and slalom
- The Olympic Nordic stadium at Pragelato is at 1,520m altitude
- The course uses sections of the original Olympic cross-country trails
- One of the few races that connects an Olympic Nordic venue to an Olympic alpine venue
