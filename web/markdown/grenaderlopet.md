## History

Founded in 1975, Grenaderløpet is one of Norway's longest single-day classic ski races at 90km through the Buskerud region. The race serves as an official seeding event for Birkebeinerrennet, making it a key early-season target for Norwegian long-distance skiers. The name references the Grenadier military tradition of the region. The race has built a loyal following among Norwegian endurance skiers seeking the ultimate single-day test.

### Notable Facts

- Name references the Grenadier military tradition of the Buskerud region
