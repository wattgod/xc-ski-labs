## Climate

Pacific coastal mountain winter

Whistler Olympic Park sits at 850m elevation in the Callaghan Valley. February temperatures range from -10°C to 2°C. Heavy Pacific snowfall ensures deep snowpack, but proximity to the coast means occasional warm intrusions and wet snow. The Callaghan Valley is more sheltered and colder than Whistler Village.

Typical race-day temperature: -10 to 2°C

Challenges:
- Pacific warm fronts can bring rain or wet, heavy snow
- Altitude gain from valley floor adds temperature and snow variability
- Klister conditions possible in warm spells
- Heavy snowfall can dump 30cm+ overnight, softening the track

## History

Founded in 2011, Payakentsut was created to activate the 2010 Vancouver Winter Olympics cross-country ski venue as a citizen race destination. The Whistler Olympic Park in the Callaghan Valley features world-class trail infrastructure built for Olympic competition. The race name honors the indigenous heritage of the region. Despite its youth, the event benefits from Olympic-level grooming, timing, and venue management.

### Notable Facts

- Uses the 2010 Vancouver Winter Olympics cross-country ski venue
- Whistler Olympic Park is operated by the Resort Municipality of Whistler
- The Callaghan Valley is separate from Whistler Village — quieter and more remote
- Olympic biathlon and cross-country events were held on these trails
- One of the youngest major marathon ski events in Canada
- The venue regularly hosts national-level and World Cup junior competitions
