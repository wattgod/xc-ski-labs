## Course

A 36km multi-lap freestyle criterium at 1,600m altitude in Sportgastein. The course features 'The Wall' — punishing climbs that test pure climbing power at altitude. Multi-lap format enables tactical racing with intermediate sprint and climbing competitions for bonus points. One of the most demanding early-season tests on the Ski Classics calendar.

## History

The Bad Gastein Criterium is the Ski Classics Pro Tour season opener, held at Sportgastein at 1,600m altitude in the Salzburg Alps. The multi-lap criterium format features 'The Wall' — iconic climbs that test pure power at altitude — alongside tactical sprint and climbing competitions. The 2025/2026 Season XVII opener saw Kasper Stadaas and Anikken Gjerde Alnaes take the victories. The event has been voted among the best on the Ski Classics calendar.

### Notable Facts

- Ski Classics Pro Tour season opener
- Held at 1,600m altitude in Sportgastein, Salzburg Alps
- 'The Wall' — iconic climbing sections that define the race
- Multi-lap criterium format with sprint and climbing bonus competitions
- Season XVII (2025/2026) winners: Kasper Stadaas and Anikken Gjerde Alnaes
- Part of the Bad Gastein weekend alongside the Pro Team Tempo event
