## History

Sun Valley has been a skiing destination since 1936 when the Union Pacific Railroad built the Sun Valley Lodge as America's first destination ski resort. The Nordic center carries on that tradition, offering some of the finest groomed trails in the American West. The Nordic Festival celebrates the area's deep skiing culture.

### Notable Facts

- Sun Valley was America's first destination ski resort (1936)
- The Nordic Center maintains over 40km of groomed trails
- Ernest Hemingway was a famous Sun Valley resident and skier
- The area regularly hosts USSA and junior national Nordic competitions
