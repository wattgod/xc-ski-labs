## Course

60km through the dense boreal forests of inland Jämtland. Rolling terrain with long forested stretches and occasional lake crossings. A wilderness experience with minimal spectator access along much of the route.

Format: point-to-point

## Climate

Interior Scandinavian continental

Jämtland's interior location produces cold, stable winters with reliable snow cover. Continental climate means colder temperatures than coastal areas, with calm conditions in the forested valleys.

Typical race-day temperature: -15 to -5°C

Challenges:
- Cold temperatures in sheltered forest valleys
- Long distance through remote terrain
- Limited daylight in early March
- Wax selection in cold, dry conditions

## History

The Jämtland Ski Marathon is a 60km classic race through the interior forests of Jämtland, one of Sweden's most sparsely populated regions. The race showcases the deep boreal wilderness of inland Scandinavia, offering a dramatically different experience from the crowded Vasaloppet corridor further south.

### Notable Facts

- Traverses some of Sweden's most remote boreal forest
- Jämtland is one of Sweden's least populated regions
- Offers a wilderness alternative to more commercial Swedish loppets
- Course passes through terrain frequented by moose, lynx, and wolverine
