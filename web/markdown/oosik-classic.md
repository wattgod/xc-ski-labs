## History

The Oosik Classic is a citizen ski marathon hosted by the Denali Nordic Ski Club in Talkeetna, Alaska. The race is the club's biggest annual fundraiser, drawing skiers from across the state. Uniquely, the course changes every year, providing new terrain and a fresh experience annually. Starting at high noon in the breathtaking Alaskan backcountry near Denali, it embodies Alaska's independent, adventure-first skiing culture.

### Notable Facts

- Course changes every year — always new terrain
- Hosted by the Denali Nordic Ski Club in Talkeetna, Alaska
- Club's biggest annual fundraiser event
- Starts at high noon — unique timing for a ski marathon
- Near Denali (formerly Mt. McKinley) — North America's highest peak
- Classic technique only — Alaskan backcountry skiing tradition
- Three distance options from 10km to 50km
