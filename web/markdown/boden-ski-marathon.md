## Climate

Subarctic inland, cold and stable

Boden's position in interior Norrbotten produces cold, stable winters with reliable snow cover from November through April. March temperatures typically -15°C to -5°C. Dry continental air from the east keeps snow quality excellent.

Typical race-day temperature: -15 to -5°C

Challenges:
- Consistent cold temperatures require proper layering
- Wind exposure on open sections near military training grounds
- Remote northern location adds travel logistics
- Early March daylight improving but still limited

## History

Founded in 1978 in Boden, Sweden's largest garrison town, where the military presence has maintained extensive trail infrastructure. Boden Fortress, built in the early 1900s as Sweden's northern defense, provides a unique historical backdrop. The marathon has become Norrbotten's accessible alternative to more remote Arctic races.

### Notable Facts

- Boden is Sweden's largest garrison town — military skiing has deep roots here
- Boden Fortress was Sweden's most expensive military construction, declassified in 1998
- The town sits at the confluence of the Lule River
- Norrbotten county is Sweden's largest by area, smallest by population density
