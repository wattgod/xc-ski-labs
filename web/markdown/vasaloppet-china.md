## Course

Loop course through Jingyuetan National Forest Park on the outskirts of Changchun. The park's frozen lake and dense pine forest create a scenic setting on gently undulating terrain.

Format: loop  
Surface: Machine-groomed classic tracks through national forest park

### Features

- Set in Jingyuetan National Forest Park — one of Asia's largest urban forest parks
- Licensed by the Swedish Vasaloppet organization
- Worldloppet member since 2014
- Frozen Jingyuetan (Clear Moon Lake) as course centerpiece
- China's flagship cross-country ski marathon

## Climate

Continental Northeast Asian winter

Changchun's extreme continental climate delivers some of the coldest conditions in the Worldloppet circuit. January temperatures range from -25°C to -10°C. Siberian air masses dominate, producing dry, cold snow. Wind chill can be severe across open sections of the forest park.

Typical race-day temperature: -22 to -10°C

Challenges:
- Extreme cold — among the coldest Worldloppet venues
- Frostbite risk on exposed skin at -25°C and below
- Dry cold affects breathing and hydration
- Limited course familiarity for most international visitors

## History

Founded in 2003 as a collaboration between the Swedish Vasaloppet organization and Chinese skiing authorities, Vasaloppet China was created to promote cross-country skiing in Northeast China. Set in Changchun's Jingyuetan National Forest Park, the event became a Worldloppet member in 2014. It represents China's ambitions in Nordic skiing, boosted by the country's 2022 Winter Olympics hosting and government push to engage 300 million people in winter sports.

### Notable Facts

- Licensed directly by the Swedish Vasaloppet organization
- Worldloppet member since 2014
- Set in Jingyuetan National Forest Park — 96 square kilometers of protected forest
- Benefited from China's push to engage 300 million people in winter sports
- One of the coldest venues on the Worldloppet circuit
