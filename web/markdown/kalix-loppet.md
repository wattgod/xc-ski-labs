## History

Kalix Loppet is a regional classic-technique ski marathon held in the town of Kalix on Sweden's northern Bothnian coast, near the Arctic Circle. The Kalix River is one of Sweden's last undammed rivers.

### Notable Facts

- Kalix lies near the Arctic Circle at 66°N latitude
- The Kalix River is one of Sweden's four remaining undammed rivers
- Gulf of Bothnia freezes over in most winters
- Small but dedicated Norrbotten skiing community
