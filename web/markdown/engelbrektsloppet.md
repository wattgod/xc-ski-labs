## Climate

Continental Swedish winter

Bergslagen's inland position typically delivers reliable cold and good snow conditions in early February. Temperatures range from -15°C to -3°C. The forested terrain provides shelter from wind but can trap cold air in valleys.

## History

Founded in 1969 and named after Engelbrekt Engelbrektsson, a 15th-century Swedish rebel leader from the Bergslagen mining region. The race quickly established itself as Sweden's second-largest ski marathon behind Vasaloppet. Its inclusion in En Svensk Klassiker — a four-race endurance challenge combining skiing, cycling, running, and swimming — has cemented its place in Swedish sporting culture.

### Notable Facts

- Named after Engelbrekt Engelbrektsson, who led a peasant revolt in 1434
- Second-largest ski race in Sweden, behind only Vasaloppet
- One of four events in En Svensk Klassiker (The Swedish Classic)
- Course traverses the historic iron-mining region of Bergslagen
- Many participants use it as a Vasaloppet warm-up race
