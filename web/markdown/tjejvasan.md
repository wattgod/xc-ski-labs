## Course

The second half of the iconic Vasaloppet trail, from Oxberg to Mora. Rolling forest terrain with gentle climbs and descents through birch and pine forests. The final kilometers cross open farmland before the historic finish in Mora.

## History

Founded in 1988 as a women-only complement to Vasaloppet, Tjejvasan covers the final 30 kilometers of the historic Vasaloppet trail from Oxberg to Mora. It has grown into the world's largest women-only ski race, with nearly 8,000 participants annually. The event is a cornerstone of Vasaloppet Winter Week and has become a powerful symbol of women's participation in Nordic skiing.

### Notable Facts

- World's largest women-only cross-country ski race

- Shares the final 30km of the Vasaloppet course, finishing in Mora
