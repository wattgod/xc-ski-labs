## History

The Pacific Northwest Loppet Series brings community Nordic racing to various venues across Oregon's Cascade Range. The series promotes participation-focused skiing in one of the snowiest mountain ranges in North America.

### Notable Facts

- Oregon's Cascades receive some of the deepest snowpack in North America
- Multiple venues showcase different Cascade landscapes
- Pacific Northwest Nordic community is growing steadily
- Mt. Bachelor and other volcanic peaks provide dramatic backdrops
