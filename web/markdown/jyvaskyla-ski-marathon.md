## Climate

Finnish Lakeland winter

March in Central Finland means late winter with lengthening days. Inland location provides consistent cold. Temperatures typically -10°C to -2°C. Snow is very reliable in the Finnish interior at this time of year. The frozen lake landscape is characteristic.

Typical race-day temperature: -10 to -2°C

Challenges:
- Open lake crossings expose skiers to wind
- Low altitude means flat terrain — relentless aerobic demand
- March sun can soften tracks in afternoon
- Finnish cold demands steady nutrition strategy over 50km

## History

A 50km ski marathon in Jyväskylä, Central Finland, offering both classic and freestyle disciplines. Part of the Euroloppet series. The race follows the Metso Route through the Finnish Lakeland, one of the world's largest lake systems. Jyväskylä is a major university city and cultural center in Central Finland, providing excellent infrastructure for the event. Attracts around 1,100 participants.

### Notable Facts
