## Course

Point-to-point through sub-Arctic terrain with sustained climbing that makes double-poling virtually impossible. The course demands genuine classical technique — diagonal stride, herringbone, and kick-and-glide are essential. Rolling hills through birch forest and open tundra.

Format: point-to-point

## Climate

Arctic Scandinavian winter

Situated above the Arctic Circle at 69°N, temperatures on race day can range from -20°C to -5°C. Arctic wind chill is a major factor across exposed sections. Snow conditions are typically cold and fast, but polar weather systems can bring sudden whiteouts.

Typical race-day temperature: -15 to -5°C

Challenges:

## History

Founded in 1964 in the Troms region of northern Norway, Reistadløpet has become one of the most respected classical skiing races in Scandinavia. Its inclusion in the Ski Classics series elevated its international profile. The race is named after the Reistad area near Bardufoss and is celebrated for its uncompromising terrain that rewards traditional diagonal-stride technique.

### Notable Facts

- Northernmost race in the Ski Classics series at 69°N latitude
- Course design makes double-poling nearly impossible — a rarity in modern marathon skiing
- Located near a Norwegian military base in Bardufoss
- Has become a cult favorite among purist classical skiers
- Part of the Ski Classics Pro Tour since the series began
