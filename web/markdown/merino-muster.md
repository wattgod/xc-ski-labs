## History

Founded in 1995 at the Snow Farm, New Zealand's only dedicated Nordic ski area in the Pisa Range above Cardrona Valley. The Merino Muster takes its name from the sheep musters that have defined high-country life in Otago for over 150 years. As the only Worldloppet race in the Southern Hemisphere, it occupies a unique position in the global marathon skiing calendar, offering Northern Hemisphere skiers a counter-season race in one of the most remote and scenic ski settings on Earth.

### Notable Facts

- Only Worldloppet race in the Southern Hemisphere
- Named after the merino sheep musters of Otago's high country
- Set at Snow Farm — New Zealand's only Nordic ski area
- Counter-season timing draws Northern Hemisphere skiers in August-September
- Smallest field in the Worldloppet series at approximately 100 starters
- The Pisa Range setting offers views toward Queenstown and Wanaka
