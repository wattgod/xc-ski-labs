## Course

A 440km seven-stage guided ski tour crossing Finland from the Russian border to the Swedish border near the Arctic Circle. Daily stages range from 44km to 84km through fells, forests, and frozen rivers. The course traverses the full width of Finnish Lapland through remote wilderness terrain. This is a guided adventure event, not a traditional timed race.

## History

Rajalta Rajalle Hiihto (Border to Border Ski) is an extreme multi-day guided ski tour crossing the full width of Finland from the Russian border to the Swedish border near the Arctic Circle. Seven days of skiing at daily distances of 44-84km totaling 440km through Lapland wilderness. Not a traditional race but a guided adventure event, it has built growing international popularity among ultra-endurance Nordic skiers seeking the ultimate multi-day ski challenge.

### Notable Facts

- 440km crossing of Finland — Russian border to Swedish border
- Seven stages over seven consecutive days
- Daily distances range from 44km to 84km
- Guided adventure event — not a traditional timed race
- One of the longest multi-day ski events in the world
- Route traverses remote Lapland wilderness near the Arctic Circle
- Growing international participation from ultra-endurance skiers
