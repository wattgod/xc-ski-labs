## Course

Loop course through the Haanja Uplands in southern Estonia, home to the Baltic states' highest point (Suur Munamägi, 318m). Despite the modest altitude, the terrain is surprisingly hilly with constant rolling elevation changes. The course winds through mixed forest and open farmland in a landscape shaped by glacial moraines.

## History

The Haanja Maraton is the second-oldest marathon ski race in Estonia and a key event in the Estoloppet series — Estonia's national citizen marathon ski series. Set in the Haanja Uplands of southern Estonia, the race takes place in the hilliest terrain in the Baltic states. Estonia has a deep Nordic skiing tradition, with cross-country skiing serving as both a national sport and a practical winter transportation method for centuries in rural areas.
