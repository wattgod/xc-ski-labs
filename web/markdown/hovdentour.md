## Climate

Southern Norwegian mountain winter

Hovden sits at approximately 800m in the Setesdal mountains of Southern Norway. The altitude provides reliable snow conditions from December through April. Race day temperatures typically range from -12°C to -2°C.

## History

HovdenTour has been a fixture of the Southern Norwegian ski calendar since approximately the 1990s. Hovden is one of Southern Norway's premier ski destinations, located in the Setesdal mountains of Agder county. The race leverages the resort's extensive 170km trail network to offer a mountain ski marathon accessible from Southern Norway's population centers.
