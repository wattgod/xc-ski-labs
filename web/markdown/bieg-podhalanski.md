## Climate

Carpathian mountain winter

February in the Tatra foothills at 900m brings reliable Carpathian winter conditions. Temperatures typically -10°C to 0°C. The Tatras act as a snow barrier, receiving heavy precipitation from southern weather systems. The Podhale plateau retains snow well.

Typical race-day temperature: -10 to 0°C

Challenges:
- Föhn wind (halny) can bring sudden warming from the south
- Variable conditions in the Carpathian climate

## History

The Bieg Podhalański is a ski marathon in the Podhale region of southern Poland, at the foot of the Tatra Mountains. The Podhale is home to the Góral people — Poland's highland culture with distinctive music, architecture, and traditions. Zakopane, nearby, is Poland's undisputed winter capital and has hosted numerous international ski competitions. The race celebrates the region's deep connection to winter sports and mountain culture.

### Notable Facts

- Podhale is home to the Góral people — Poland's highland mountain culture
- Near Zakopane — Poland's winter capital since the 19th century
- The Tatra Mountains are the highest range in the Carpathians
- Zakopane hosted the 1929 and 1939 FIS Nordic World Ski Championships
- Poland's Bieg Piastów (Jakuszyce) is the country's largest ski marathon — Bieg Podhalański offers the Tatra alternative
- The Podhale's traditional wooden architecture is a UNESCO-recognized heritage
