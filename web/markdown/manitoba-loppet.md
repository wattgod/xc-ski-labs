## History

The Manitoba Loppet is a community Nordic ski event in Birds Hill Provincial Park near Winnipeg, organized by the Cross Country Ski Association of Manitoba. It represents prairie skiing at its most fundamental.

### Notable Facts

- One of the few Nordic ski events on the Canadian prairies
- Organized by the Cross Country Ski Association of Manitoba
- Birds Hill Provincial Park is Winnipeg's primary Nordic venue
