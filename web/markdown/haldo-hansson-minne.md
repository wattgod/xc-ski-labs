## History

Founded in 1946 as a memorial race for Haldo Hansson (1891-1944), a legendary skier from Åsarna in Jämtland who was one of Sweden's finest cross-country competitors in the early 20th century. The race has been organized by Åsarna IF continuously and remains one of Sweden's most historically significant ski marathons, deeply rooted in the Jämtland skiing tradition.

### Notable Facts

- Haldo Hansson was a dominant Swedish skier in the 1910s and 1920s
- One of Sweden's oldest continuously held ski marathons
- Åsarna IF, the organizing club, was founded in 1908
- Jämtland has produced many of Sweden's greatest cross-country skiers
