## Climate

Alpine continental

Early February in the Bavarian Alps brings cold, snowy conditions at 850m elevation. The Ammergau Alps provide a reliable snow pocket, though climate change has introduced variability. Clear days offer stunning mountain views; snowfall during the race is common.

Typical race-day temperature: -8 to 2°C

Challenges:
- Alpine weather can shift rapidly — snowfall, fog, or sunshine within hours
- Elevation changes create micro-climate wax challenges
- Occasional warm Föhn winds can soften snow dramatically
- Cold valley inversions possible in early morning

## History

König Ludwig Lauf was founded in 1968 in Oberammergau, the Bavarian village famous for its Passion Play tradition dating to 1634. Named after King Ludwig II of Bavaria — the eccentric 'fairy-tale king' who built Neuschwanstein Castle — the race has grown into Germany's premier marathon cross-country ski event and a key fixture on the Worldloppet circuit. The Ammergau Alps region has a deep skiing heritage, and the race draws heavily from Germany's large recreational skiing community.

### Notable Facts

- Named after King Ludwig II, Bavaria's legendary castle-building monarch
- Oberammergau is famous for its Passion Play, performed every 10 years since 1634
- Germany's largest marathon cross-country ski race
- Worldloppet member since the series' early years
- The nearby Ettal Monastery provides a dramatic cultural backdrop
- Ludwig II was an avid outdoorsman who built Alpine retreats throughout the region
