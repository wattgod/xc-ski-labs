## Course

A 50km classic course through the Šumava National Park, one of the last great wilderness areas in Central Europe. The route traverses the high plateau of the Bohemian Forest, passing through ancient spruce forests and open mountain meadows. The terrain is rolling with moderate climbs, characteristic of the Šumava highland landscape.

Format: loop  
Surface: Machine-groomed classic tracks through national park forest trails  
Technical rating: 2/5  
Grooming: Good regional grooming through well-maintained national park trail network

## Climate

Bohemian mountain winter

Late January in the Šumava mountains along the Czech-German-Austrian border. The forested plateau at around 900-1100m provides reasonable snow reliability for Central Europe. Temperatures typically -10 to -4 degrees Celsius. The remote location and dense forest create a wilderness atmosphere.

Typical race-day temperature: -10 to -4°C

Challenges:
- Remote forest location with limited rescue access
- Cold air pooling in the high plateau clearings
- Variable snow quality between exposed and forested sections
- Long approach drives to the remote start location

## History

Founded in 1978 in the Šumava mountains during the Czechoslovak era. The race takes place in what was once part of the Iron Curtain exclusion zone, an area that was off-limits to most civilians for decades. This enforced isolation preserved the ancient Bohemian Forest as one of Europe's most pristine wilderness areas. After the fall of communism, the Šumava Loppet became accessible to all and now draws skiers seeking a genuine wilderness Nordic experience.

### Notable Facts

- The Šumava is a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve and one of Europe's last great forests
- Parts of the course pass through former Iron Curtain exclusion zones
- Kvílda holds the Czech Republic's cold temperature records
- The Bohemian Forest straddles the Czech-German-Austrian triple border
- The Šumava National Park protects over 680 square kilometers of ancient forest
