## Course

A 25km skate-only race on the groomed trail system in the Eau Claire area. The course follows rolling terrain through hardwood forest and along the Chippewa River valley. Relatively flat by Nordic racing standards, favoring strong skaters.

Format: loop

## History

A community-organized skate ski race in Eau Claire, Wisconsin, part of the state's vibrant citizen Nordic racing culture. The race draws skiers from across western Wisconsin and the Twin Cities metro area for a fast, flat course on well-groomed trails.

### Notable Facts

- One of the few skate-only Nordic races in Wisconsin
- Eau Claire has a growing Nordic ski community
- Draws participants from the Twin Cities metro area
