## Course

A 27km freestyle course through the impressive mountain landscape of Kandersteg in the Bernese Oberland. Two rounds of a loop plus a starting section, with mass start from Mullermatte. The course is set beneath the towering Bluemlisalp massif and Oeschinensee area — some of Switzerland's most dramatic Alpine scenery.

Format: multi-loop  
Surface: Machine-groomed Alpine valley trails

## Climate

Alpine valley winter

February in Kandersteg at 1,200m in the Bernese Oberland means reliable snow and cold Alpine conditions. Temperatures range from -12°C to 2°C. The narrow valley setting provides shelter from extreme winds but can limit sun exposure. Consistent cold preserves excellent trail conditions.

Typical race-day temperature: -12 to 2°C

Challenges:
- Alpine valley cold — sub-zero temperatures common
- Limited sun exposure in narrow Kandersteg valley
- Altitude at 1,200m — mild performance effect
- Occasional Fohn wind can bring rapid warming

## History

The Kandersteger Volkslauf is a Swiss Loppet series race set in the spectacular mountain village of Kandersteg in the Bernese Oberland. The race takes place beneath the towering Bluemlisalp massif, near the UNESCO World Heritage Jungfrau-Aletsch region. Mass start from Mullermatte, the course covers two rounds of a loop through one of Switzerland's most dramatic Alpine Nordic skiing settings.
