## History

Named after Herman 'Jackrabbit' Johannsen (1875-1987), the Norwegian-born pioneer who is credited with popularizing cross-country skiing in Canada. Johannsen blazed many of the original trails through the Laurentians and skied competitively until well past his 100th birthday. The race celebrates his legacy and the deep French-Canadian ski culture of the Laurentians north of Montreal.

### Notable Facts

- Herman 'Jackrabbit' Johannsen lived to 111 years old and skied past age 100
- Johannsen blazed many of the original trails through the Laurentians in the 1920s-30s
- The Laurentians are the traditional heartland of Quebec Nordic skiing
- The race is conducted primarily in French, reflecting the region's culture
- Johannsen was inducted into the Canadian Ski Hall of Fame and is considered the father of Canadian skiing
- The Laurentian trails connect historic villages north of Montreal
