## History

Founded in 1985, the Muskoka Loppet has been a fixture of the Ontario Nordic skiing community for four decades. Held in Arrowhead Provincial Park near Huntsville, the race takes advantage of the park's well-maintained trail system and the Muskoka region's reliable winter conditions. The classic-only format has remained a deliberate choice, preserving the traditional character of the event. The race draws participants from across Ontario and beyond, serving as one of the province's premier Nordic competitions.

### Notable Facts

- Over 40 years of continuous operation in Arrowhead Provincial Park
- Classic-only format preserves traditional Nordic skiing ethos
- Arrowhead Provincial Park is known for its natural ice skating trail and winter recreation
- The Muskoka region is one of Ontario's most popular four-season recreation areas
- The race has been a qualifier and stepping stone for Canadian long-distance skiers
