## Climate

Baltic continental winter

February in the Vidzeme Uplands means cold continental conditions with reliable snow in most years. Temperatures range from -15°C to -2°C. Latvia's inland position east of Riga provides colder, more stable winter conditions than the coast. Flat terrain means wind exposure across open fields.
