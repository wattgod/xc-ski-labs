## Course

Predominantly flat to gently rolling course through the forests and coastal lowlands around Oulu. The route traverses boreal pine forest and open marshland near the Gulf of Bothnia. The flat terrain rewards endurance and double-poling strength over technical ability.

Format: point-to-point  
Surface: Machine-groomed trails through boreal forest and coastal terrain  
Technical rating: 2/5  
Grooming: Good Finnish grooming standards with well-set classic and skate tracks

### Features

- World's oldest continuously held cross-country ski race (since 1889)
- 130+ years of unbroken tradition
- Course through the boreal forests near the Gulf of Bothnia
- Both classic and freestyle 70km options
- 'Terva' means tar — Oulu was historically Finland's tar-trading capital
- Strong northern Finnish skiing culture and community
- Flat terrain rewards aerobic fitness and double-poling power

## Climate

Northern Finnish winter

February in Oulu at 65°N latitude delivers deep, reliable winter. The Gulf of Bothnia coastline moderates temperatures slightly compared to inland Finland, but conditions remain firmly below freezing. Daylight is increasing from the polar winter minimum but still limited. Snow cover is consistent and deep.

Typical race-day temperature: -15 to -5°C

Challenges:
- Northern latitude means limited February daylight for back-of-pack skiers
- Coastal winds from the Gulf of Bothnia can create wind chill
- Flat terrain means sustained effort without recovery descents
- 70km distance at sub-minus-10 demands careful cold management
