## Climate

Sub-Arctic continental

The Tornedalen valley along the Swedish-Finnish border experiences some of Scandinavia's coldest temperatures. Deep cold is the norm, with stable snow cover well into April. Flat river terrain offers little shelter from Arctic winds.

Typical race-day temperature: -25 to -10°C

Challenges:
- Extreme cold — temperatures can drop below -30°C
- Wind exposure on the open frozen river
- 100km ultra distance in Arctic conditions
- Limited support infrastructure in remote sections
- Frostbite risk requires careful gear preparation

## History

The Tornedalen Ski Marathon follows the frozen Torne River along the Swedish-Finnish border in the sub-Arctic north. At 100km, it is one of the longest ski marathons in Scandinavia and among the coldest. The Tornedalen (Torne Valley) is home to the Meänkieli-speaking Tornedalian minority, and the race traverses their traditional homeland.

### Notable Facts

- Follows the frozen Torne River along the Swedish-Finnish border
- 100km makes it one of the longest ski marathons in Scandinavia
- Traverses the traditional homeland of the Tornedalian minority
- Temperatures regularly drop below -25°C during the race
- The Torne River forms the natural border between Sweden and Finland
