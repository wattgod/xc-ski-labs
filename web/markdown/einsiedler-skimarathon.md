## History

The Einsiedler Skimarathon was founded in 1969 and is the most traditional race in the Swiss Loppet series. Held in the pre-Alpine town of Einsiedeln, famous for its Benedictine abbey and centuries of pilgrimage tradition, the race has been a cornerstone of Swiss citizen cross-country skiing for nearly six decades. The 58th edition takes place in 2026.
