## Course

Straight-line crossing of frozen Chequamegon Bay on Lake Superior from Ashland to Washburn. Completely flat, open ice surface lit by bonfires.

Format: point-to-point  
Surface: Groomed track on frozen lake ice  
Technical rating: 1/5  
Grooming: Basic grooming on ice

### Features

- Night crossing of frozen Lake Superior bay
- Bonfires line the route across the ice
- Unique community event atmosphere
- Participants can ski, snowshoe, or walk
- Shuttle service back to start

## History

Book Across the Bay began in 1996 as a nighttime community ski event crossing frozen Chequamegon Bay on Lake Superior. It has grown into one of Wisconsin's most popular winter events, drawing thousands of participants who ski, snowshoe, or walk the 10km route lit by bonfires.

### Notable Facts

- Night crossing of frozen Lake Superior bay
- Bonfires light the route across the ice
- Open to skiers, snowshoers, and walkers
- Founded 1996 — grown to 3,000+ participants
- Has been cancelled in years when bay ice is insufficient
