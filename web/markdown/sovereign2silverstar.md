Point-to-point from Sovereign Lake Nordic Centre to Silver Star Village. The route traverses high-altitude plateau terrain through subalpine forest at 1,600m. Rolling terrain with moderate climbing and a festive downhill finish into Silver Star Village.

## Climate

Interior BC highland spring

April at 1,600m elevation in the Okanagan Highland delivers spring skiing conditions. Temperatures range from -5°C to 8°C. Snow is usually corn or spring consolidated. Bright sun and warm afternoons are the norm — sunscreen matters more than wind protection.

Typical race-day temperature: -5 to 8°C

Challenges:
- Spring snow conditions — soft, slow corn snow by afternoon
- Intense high-altitude UV requires serious sun protection
- Course can get slushy in warm years, especially south-facing sections
- Late-season snow coverage not always guaranteed for full distance

## History

Founded around 2010, Sovereign to Silver Star connects two of British Columbia's premier Nordic ski centres with a point-to-point marathon. The race's tagline — 'Point to party' — captures its spirit: a challenging 42km traverse through high-altitude Okanagan Highland forest followed by a rollicking finish-line celebration in Silver Star Village. The April timing makes it one of the latest-season marathon ski races in North America, embracing spring corn snow and sunshine.

### Notable Facts

- Connects Sovereign Lake and Silver Star — two of BC's top Nordic centres
- Tagline: 'Point to party' — finish-line celebration is a major draw
- At 1,600m, one of the highest-altitude marathon ski races in Canada
- April timing makes it one of the last marathon ski events of the North American season
- Capped at 1,200 participants for trail and logistics management
- Both Nordic centres are known for exceptional natural snowfall
