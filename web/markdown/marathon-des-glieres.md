## History

The Marathon des Glières, founded around 1991, takes place on one of France's most historically significant sites. The Plateau des Glières was a stronghold of the French Resistance during World War II — in March 1944, nearly 500 maquisards held the plateau against Vichy and German forces. A massive stone monument on the plateau commemorates their sacrifice. The ski marathon honors this legacy, combining sport with remembrance on the same ground where resistance fighters operated on skis.

### Notable Facts

- Plateau des Glières was a French Resistance stronghold in WWII
- In March 1944, ~500 maquisards defended the plateau against German and Vichy forces
- The National Resistance Monument stands on the plateau
- Skiers literally race on the same ground where resistance fighters fought
- Located in Haute-Savoie near the lakeside city of Annecy
- Part of the broader French marathon ski calendar
