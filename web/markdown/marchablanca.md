## Course

A 21km course through the Tierra del Fuego landscape near Ushuaia, the world's southernmost city. The course traverses subantarctic forest and open terrain at approximately 300m altitude. Moderate rolling terrain with views of the Martial Glacier and Beagle Channel. A shorter 7.5km promotional distance is also offered.

## Climate

Patagonian subantarctic winter

August in Ushuaia at 54°S latitude means deep Southern Hemisphere winter. Temperatures range from -5°C to 5°C. Tierra del Fuego's maritime subantarctic climate provides consistent but often wet and windy conditions. Snow can be heavy and wind-affected. Daylight is limited — about 9 hours in August at this extreme southern latitude.

Typical race-day temperature: -5 to 5°C

Challenges:
- Subantarctic wind — Tierra del Fuego is notoriously windy
- Wet maritime snow — heavier than continental powder
- Limited August daylight at 54°S latitude
- Remote southern location — limited international accessibility
- Variable snow quality due to maritime influence

## History

The Marchablanca has been South America's most important cross-country ski race since its founding in 1986. Organized by the Club Andino Ushuaia (CAU), a nonprofit institution nearly sixty years old, the race takes place in Tierra del Fuego near Ushuaia — the world's southernmost city. The event has been part of the Worldloppet candidacy process alongside the Ushuaia Loppet, seeking to bring South American citizen skiing into the global Worldloppet family. With up to 780 entries in its peak years, it represents the heart of Argentine cross-country skiing culture.
