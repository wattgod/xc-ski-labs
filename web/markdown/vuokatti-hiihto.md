## Course

Rolling terrain through boreal forest and lake country around Vuokatti hill. The course uses the extensive trail network of Vuokatti's world-class Nordic ski center.

Format: loop

- Year-round ski tunnel nearby for pre-race training

## History

Vuokatti Hiihto takes advantage of one of Finland's premier Nordic training centers in Sotkamo. The Vuokatti area has invested heavily in snowmaking and year-round training facilities including a ski tunnel, making it a hub for both amateur and professional Nordic skiing.

### Notable Facts

- Held at one of Finland's premier Nordic training centers
- Vuokatti ski tunnel allows year-round training
- Kainuu region is known for reliable winter conditions
- Growing event attracting Finnish and international participants
