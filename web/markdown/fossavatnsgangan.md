## Climate

Subarctic maritime

The Westfjords deliver unpredictable late-April conditions. Temperatures range from -5°C to 8°C. Atlantic storms can bring whiteout blizzards or sudden warm spells. Snow cover at this latitude persists well into spring, but maritime influence creates heavy, wet snow conditions.

Typical race-day temperature: -3 to 5°C

Challenges:
- Extreme weather variability — blizzards to sunshine in hours
- Atlantic storms with high winds and whiteout conditions
- Wet, heavy spring snow difficult for waxing
- Remote location complicates evacuation in emergencies

## History

Founded in 1935, Fossavatnsgangan is one of the oldest organized ski races in the world and the most northerly event in the Worldloppet series. The race takes its name from Fossavatn (Waterfall Lake) in the Westfjords. For nearly a century, Icelanders and international visitors have raced across this remote volcanic landscape, making it a bucket-list event for Worldloppet passport holders seeking the most extreme and unique race settings.

### Notable Facts
