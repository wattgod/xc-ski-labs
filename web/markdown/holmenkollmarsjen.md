## History

Founded in 1974, Holmenkollmarsjen was created to give recreational skiers a mass-start marathon finishing at Norway's most hallowed ski venue — the Holmenkollen ski jump. The course traverses the Nordmarka forest that has been central to Oslo skiing culture for over a century. As part of the Ski Classics Challengers series, it attracts both elite long-distance racers and enthusiastic amateurs tackling one of Scandinavia's most elevation-heavy marathon courses.

### Notable Facts

- Finishes at Holmenkollen, site of the 1952 Olympics and annual World Cup events
- Over 1,000 meters of climbing makes it one of the toughest Scandinavian marathons
- Part of the Visma Ski Classics Challengers series
- The Nordmarka forest has been Oslo's skiing playground since the 1800s
- Attracts strong Norwegian domestic field plus international Ski Classics followers
