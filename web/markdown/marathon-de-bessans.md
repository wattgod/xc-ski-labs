## Course

A multi-loop course through the high Maurienne valley at 1,750 meters altitude. The terrain is rolling with moderate climbs through alpine meadows and sparse larch forests. Surrounded by glaciated peaks of the Vanoise National Park. The altitude is the defining challenge — sustained effort at nearly 6,000 feet.

Format: loop  
Surface: Machine-groomed tracks on high-altitude alpine plateau  
Technical rating: 3/5  
Grooming: French national team training venue — excellent grooming standards

## Climate

High alpine continental

Mid-January at 1,750 meters in the Maurienne valley means intense cold and reliable deep snow. Temperatures range from -18°C to -5°C. The high altitude guarantees snow conditions but the cold demands serious preparation. Clear skies bring stunning views but also rapid radiant cooling.

Typical race-day temperature: -18 to -5°C

Challenges:
- Extreme cold at 1,750m altitude in January
- Altitude effects on performance — thin air at race pace
- Early-season fitness test — January date catches many underprepared
- Sun-shade temperature differentials on valley loops

## History

Founded in 1979, the Marathon de Bessans has served as Europe's season-opening ski marathon for over four decades. Set at 1,750 meters in the Maurienne valley of Savoie, the event benefits from Bessans' status as a French biathlon and cross-country national team training center. The high altitude guarantees snow when lower venues struggle, making it the traditional January curtain-raiser for the European marathon circuit.

### Notable Facts

- Highest venue on the Euroloppet circuit at 1,750m
- Season opener — traditionally the first major European marathon each winter
- Bessans is a French national team training base for biathlon and XC skiing
- Founded in 1979 — over 45 years of continuous racing
- Euroloppet member event
- Adjacent to Vanoise National Park, France's first national park
