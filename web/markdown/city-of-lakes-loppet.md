## Course

Urban loop course through Theodore Wirth Park and surrounding Minneapolis lakes. The route crosses frozen lakes, winds through city parkland, and traverses some of the best urban Nordic trails in the US. Rolling terrain with short punchy climbs in the park sections.

Format: loop  
Surface: Machine-groomed trails through urban parkland and frozen lake crossings  
Technical rating: 2/5  
Grooming: Excellent urban trail grooming by Loppet Foundation

## Climate

Upper Midwest continental winter

Minneapolis in February is reliably cold with temperatures typically between -15°C and -5°C. The city's position on the northern Great Plains makes it one of the coldest major cities in the US. Snow cover is generally reliable, though warm spells can threaten conditions.

Typical race-day temperature: -15 to -5°C

Challenges:
- Urban course means variable snow quality near roads and crossings
- Wind exposure on frozen lake crossings
- Cold continental air masses can bring extreme wind chill
- Occasional warm spells threaten snow cover at low altitude

## History

Founded in 2003 by the Loppet Foundation, the City of Lakes Loppet was created to bring marathon ski racing into the urban fabric of Minneapolis. The race leverages the city's exceptional park system and chain of lakes, proving that world-class Nordic skiing can thrive in a metropolitan setting. The Loppet Foundation has since expanded into a year-round outdoor recreation organization serving the Minneapolis community.

### Notable Facts

- Founded by the Loppet Foundation, which now operates year-round outdoor programs
- Theodore Wirth Park is considered the best urban Nordic skiing venue in the US
- The Loppet Festival includes fat bike loppet, skijor loppet, and other winter events
- Minneapolis has the most extensive urban Nordic trail system in America
- The Foundation's mission connects underserved communities to outdoor recreation
- Frozen lake crossings are a signature feature of the course
