## History

Founded in 2007, the Demino Ski Marathon quickly established itself as Russia's premier citizen ski marathon. The event benefits from the Demino sports complex near Rybinsk, a purpose-built facility that has hosted FIS World Cup biathlon and cross-country events. The race joined the Worldloppet series, gaining international recognition and attracting foreign participants. However, the event's Worldloppet membership was suspended in 2022 following international sporting sanctions on Russia. The marathon continues as a domestic event, drawing approximately 2,500 participants from across Russia.
