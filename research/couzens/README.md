## Headlines from the free work

- Long-term load grows about 10% a year; the ramp is about 10 CTL a month.
- Hold one basic week for 4–8 weeks, then step up by no more than 10%.
- Never raise volume and intensity at the same time.
- Early season loads flat in 2:1 or 3:1 cycles.
- Masters: about +12 h a year just to hold fitness with age; volume is not cut.
- No ski-specific article exists on his site; his multi-sport writing is
  triathlon.

Contradictions with the XC rules are questions for Matti, never resolved in
these notes.
